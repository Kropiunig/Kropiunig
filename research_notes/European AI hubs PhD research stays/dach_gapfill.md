## Q1. Austria: TU Wien, JKU Linz, ISTA groups, Uni Vienna, TU Graz, Know-Center

### Takeaway
Austria's verified 2026 strengths are:
- ISTA: efficient LLMs (Alistarh), trustworthy/LLM-instruction safety (Lampert), learning theory (Mondelli) and differential privacy (Henzinger).
- Uni Vienna: security of LLM and agentic systems (Weippl's Security & Privacy group) and safety in multi-agent LLM systems (Pawelczyk).
- TU Graz ISEC: a new Secure AI Systems lab (Rieger, from 1 Sep 2026).
- TU Wien CAIML: symbolic AI, ML and explainable AI.

I found no formal visiting-PhD program at TU Wien, JKU, Uni Vienna or TU Graz. Visits are arranged directly with a PI and typically funded through Ernst Mach (see dach.md).

### Cited Findings
**TU Wien, CAIML (Vienna)**
- CAIML has a 12-researcher board drawn from TU Wien Informatics and Mathematics/Geoinformation. It has three pillars: symbolic AI methods, ML methods, and explainable AI / digital humanism — [CAIML](https://caiml.dbai.tuwien.ac.at); [caiml.org](https://caiml.org/)
- The 5th CAIML Symposium was held on 11 May 2026 — [TU Wien Informatics news 2026](https://informatics.tuwien.ac.at/news/filter/year/2026)
- TU Wien security: Daniel Arp (ML security) is profiled by TU Wien Informatics — [#5QW: Daniel Arp](https://informatics.tuwien.ac.at/news/2764)
- No visiting-researcher call was found. A CAIML researcher's 2025 internship in Tokyo was outbound — [CAIML news](https://caiml.dbai.tuwien.ac.at/icaiml/news/)

**JKU Linz (Institute for Machine Learning / NXAI)**
- NXAI GmbH was founded in Dec 2023 by Sepp Hochreiter and Netural X. It has a research cooperation with JKU's LIT AI Lab to bring xLSTM to market readiness — [JKU news](https://www.jku.at/news-events/detail/news/ai-made-in-europe-spitzenforscher-sepp-hochreiter-und-sein-xlstm-erhalten-unternehmerische-verstaerkung-fuer-europaeisches-large-language-model/)
- No 2026 visitor program was found, and jku.at could not be fetched.

**ISTA groups (Klosterneuburg)**
- Alistarh (DASLab) works on efficient training and inference algorithms. 2026 papers include LLMQ (low-precision LLM training on consumer GPUs), NVFP4 pre-training (Clover), WUSH (quantization), "The Geometry of LLM Quantization" and LLM sparsity via ADMM — [Alistarh group](https://ista.ac.at/en/alistarh-group/); [research explorer](https://research-explorer.ista.ac.at/groups/DaAl)
- Lampert: ASIDE (separating instructions from data in LLMs, ICLR 2026, relevant to prompt-injection safety) and federated learning with unlabeled clients (ICML 2026) — [ISTA ELLIS unit publications](https://ellis.pages.ista.ac.at/publications/)
- Mondelli: theory of pretraining and fine-tuning, weak-to-strong scaling laws, and attention sinks (co-authored with Lampert) — [ISTA ELLIS publications](https://ellis.pages.ista.ac.at/publications/)
- Henzinger: differential privacy (DP mechanisms for linear regression, NeurIPS 2026). No LLM paper was found — [ISTA Annual Report 2025 publications (PDF)](https://ista.ac.at/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ISTA_Annual_Report_2025_PUBLICATIONS.pdf)

**University of Vienna (Faculty of Computer Science)**
- The Security & Privacy group (Edgar Weippl) works at the AI × cybersecurity intersection: security of LLM-based and agentic AI systems, privacy-preserving ML, and AI for security — [sec.cs.univie.ac.at research focus](https://sec.cs.univie.ac.at/research/focus); [Weippl staff call (PDF)](https://docs.univie.ac.at/fileadmin/user_upload/p_docs/06_Pooled_Call/_05_PC-6-25_ScientificProjectStaff_Weippl.pdf)
- Martin Pawelczyk (tenure-track, Responsible ML) works on machine unlearning, privacy, and "robust alignment and safety in multi-agent LLM systems" — [Uni Vienna new professorship](https://www.univie.ac.at/en/news/new-professorships/details/pawelczyk-martin)
- Benjamin Roth's NLP group measures LLM properties against the EU AI Act and GDPR. It sits within the Data Mining & ML group (7 professors, 27 researchers) — [Faculty of CS (e-STEEM)](https://careers.univie.ac.at/en/postdoc/e-steem/faculty-of-computer-science)
- SBA Research / Uni Vienna (Daryna Oliynyk) is co-organising an adversarial-ML workshop at SaTML 2027. The source is LinkedIn, so this is unverified — [Reinhard Kugler LinkedIn](https://at.linkedin.com/in/reinhard-kugler)

**TU Graz (ISEC)**
- Phillip Rieger (PhD TU Darmstadt 2025) became Assistant Professor for Secure AI Systems and leads the SAIS Lab from 1 Sep 2026. The lab studies AI-system vulnerabilities and defences. ISEC has more than 60 information-security researchers — [ISEC announcement](https://www.isec.tugraz.at/?p=23609); [Cybersecurity & AI professorship call (PDF)](https://www.isec.tugraz.at/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/7050-2025-8413-Cybersecurity-AI.pdf)
- Bettina Könighofer (ISEC team leader) works on formal methods, safe RL/shielding, and automata learning for LLM/agentic AI — [LinkedIn](https://at.linkedin.com/in/bettina-koenighofer)

### Inferences
- For an agent-security or blockchain-security profile in Austria, the best verified matches are Uni Vienna Weippl (who also has SBA Research ties), TU Graz Rieger/ISEC and ISTA Lampert. Pawelczyk is the multi-agent LLM safety match.

## Q2. Switzerland: EPFL, IDSIA, Idiap, UZH/Basel, ETH groups, Swiss AI / CSCS

### Takeaway
EPFL's AI Center does host visiting PhDs: its Fellows & Researchers page lists them with their EPFL hosts, for example multi-agent orchestration under Kamgarpour/Cevher, LLM agents for biology under Brbic, and LLM training under Jaggi. No open call was found, so access goes through the host PI. Idiap runs internships of 6–18 months through its recruitment portal, open to non-EPFL students. IDSIA has no visiting program and only regular posts, including agentic-AI postdocs. The Swiss AI Initiative released Apertus 1.5 (8B/70B) on 24 Jul 2026, trained on CSCS Alps, and CSCS now runs an Apertus inference API for agentic applications.

### Cited Findings
**EPFL (Lausanne)**
- The AI Center Fellows & Researchers page lists visiting PhDs and their hosts. Examples: multi-agent orchestration (hosts Maryam Kamgarpour and Volkan Cevher), LLM-based agents for single-cell biology (Maria Brbic), and LLM training (Farid Adilazuarda, host Martin Jaggi) — [EPFL AI Center fellows & researchers](https://ai.epfl.ch/about/fellows-researchers/)
- On 22 May 2026 the AI Center ran a seminar, hosted by Nicolas Flammarion, on whether LLM agents can automate post-training (a benchmark). The speaker was Maksym Andriushchenko — [ai.epfl.ch](https://ai.epfl.ch/?p=18653)
- A multi-institution Swiss AI-control initiative for LLM agents is hosted at the EPFL AI Center. The source is a LinkedIn post, so this is unverified — [ai.epfl.ch research](https://ai.epfl.ch/research)
- ELLIS unit Lausanne PhD/postdoc list — [EPFL ELLIS](https://www.epfl.ch/research/domains/epfl-ellis/ellis-phd-postdoc-program/ellis-unit-lausanne-epfl-phds-postdocs)

## Q3. Germany: BIFOLD, MCML, ScaDS.AI, Lamarr, Tübingen AI Center, DFKI, Helmholtz AI, hessian.AI, others

### Takeaway
Germany has the most structured visitor schemes:
- **Lamarr Institute Visiting Researcher Program** (Bonn/Dortmund): explicitly for PhD students and postdocs, funded, by PI invitation.
- **TU Darmstadt Future Talent Guest Stay**: 1–6 weeks, PI-funded, running until 31 Dec 2026. Stays of 3 months or more need a TU Hosting Agreement.
- **Helmholtz Visiting Researcher Grant**: fully funded short stays for doctoral students and postdocs at the 18 Helmholtz centres. The last confirmed round was 2024.

MCML's AI X-Change funds only outbound exchanges for its own PhD students. DFKI routes guest inquiries to department contacts. Its multi-agent work sits mainly in Agents and Simulated Reality (Slusallek, Saarbrücken).

### Cited Findings
**Lamarr Institute (Bonn/Dortmund)**
- A Lamarr PI invites international early-career scientists (PhD students and postdocs) for funded stays at a Lamarr site, aimed at joint projects and publications. The site lists 2024–25 visitors — [Lamarr Visiting Researcher Program](https://lamarr-institute.org/visiting-researcher-program/)

**ScaDS.AI (Dresden/Leipzig)**
- The Ada Lovelace Guest Professorship is a 6–12-month stay (2025/2026) paying EUR 5,500/month, aimed at established researchers rather than PhDs — [ScaDS.AI Ada Lovelace](https://scads.ai/about-us/ada-lovelace/)

**MCML (Munich, LMU + TUM)**
- AI X-Change funds up to 12 projects abroad per year for MCML PhD students, i.e. outbound only. MCML PhD calls usually open in autumn — [MCML research x change](https://mcml.ai/opportunities/for-members/research-x-change/); [MCML admission](https://mcml.ai/opportunities/for-phd-applicants/admission-process)

**BIFOLD (Berlin)**
- BIFOLD accepts year-round job applications and runs Graduate School PhD tracks (fast-track and Master's). No visiting program was found — [BIFOLD](https://bifold.berlin)

**DFKI**
- The Agents and Simulated Reality (ASR) department (Philipp Slusallek, Saarbrücken) works on multi-agent modelling and simulation, safety engineering, and decision-making in human–AI agent teams. Matthias Klusch's I2S team (formerly co-head of a Multiagent Systems group, 2004–11) is also in Saarbrücken. There is a Cooperative and Autonomous Systems department at DFKI Niedersachsen (Osnabrück) — [DFKI](https://www.dfki.de/index.php?id=159&L=1); [Klusch research visits](https://www.dfki.de/~klusch/i2s/html/visits.html); [DFKI CAS team](https://dfki.de/en/web/research/research-departments/cooperative-and-autonomous-systems/team)

**Helmholtz AI**
- The Helmholtz Visiting Researcher Grant offers fully funded short stays at the 18 Helmholtz centres for doctoral students and postdocs in data and information science. No 2026 call was confirmed — [Research in Germany](https://research-in-germany.org/en/research-funding/funding-programmes/helmholtz-visiting-researcher-grant.html)

**hessian.AI / TU Darmstadt**
