# Southern Europe AI Hubs for Visiting PhD Research Stays (Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece, Cyprus, Malta)

Research note, compiled 2026-11-24. Method: about 20 web searches. WebFetch was blocked by the session's network allowlist for polito.it, bsc.es and software.imdea.org, so most findings come from search-result snippets of official pages, not full-page reads. Treat all details as needing confirmation on the linked page. Items marked [prior knowledge, unverified] come from background knowledge with no source found in this session; the report writer should flag or drop them.

## 1. Hubs: city, strengths, flagship groups and PIs

### Takeaway
The densest AI ecosystems are Italy (FAIR national partnership plus 5 ELLIS units: Milan, Genoa, Modena, Turin, and others) and Barcelona/Madrid in Spain. Lisbon (IST/LUMLIS + Unbabel) leads for NLP and LLMs. Athens (Archimedes/Athena RC) is the fastest-growing new hub. For the requester's interests, the strongest specific matches are IIIA-CSIC Barcelona (multi-agent systems: negotiation, trust and reputation, organisations), IMDEA Software Madrid (blockchain and crypto security, with a recurring "visiting PhD" job type), FBK Trento (security and trustworthy AI) and Archimedes Athens (Katia Sycara, multi-agent, in leadership; blockchain/economics/AI summer school).

## 2. Formal visiting PhD programs (duration, stipend, deadlines, eligibility, URLs)

### Takeaway
Few hubs run a standing, named visiting-PhD scheme. The concrete ones are BSC's Mobility Programme and HPC-Europa (short, expenses covered), IMDEA Software's periodic "visiting PhD" postings, IIT's guest-student and Erasmus+ internship routes, BCAM's visiting-fellow programme (mainly post-PhD), and Archimedes' funding of visitors. Most other stays are arranged informally with a PI and paid for by the visitor's home funding, Erasmus+ or a home-country grant.

### Cited Findings
- **BSC Mobility Programme:** open to BSC researchers and to researchers from institutions collaborating with BSC who want to visit. It funds short-term visits and covers expenses including accommodation and travel — [BSC Mobility Programmes](https://bsc.es/node/30382)
- **HPC-Europa3 (BSC-coordinated):** 3–13 week stays with travel, living costs and HPC time covered, open to researchers of any level whose work uses HPC. The current call status is unverified, because the HPC-Europa3 H2020 grant 730897 ended earlier; check whether a successor exists — [HPC-Europa3](https://hpc-europa3.bsc.es/)
- **BSC STARS** is for postdocs, not PhDs — [BSC STARS](https://bsc.es/ca/printpdf/join-us/excellence-career-opportunities/stars)
- **IIT (Genoa):** offers a "guest student" route by agreement between the IIT supervising PI and the home university, a curricular internship that needs a signed internship agreement between IIT and the home university, and Erasmus+ internship positions posted on IIT's recruitment platform (listings with June 2026 deadlines appeared on job aggregators). Spontaneous applications go to applications@iit.it. No stipend for guest students was found — [IIT students](https://iit.it/work-at-iit/career-path/students-at-iit); [IIT internship](https://iit.it/internship); [IIT Erasmus+](https://www.iit.it/en/education/erasmus-plus)

No equivalent page for Computing Sciences/AI was found. Contact phdadmission@unibocconi.it. The BIDSA VSI targets MSc students, not PhDs. ## 3. Region-specific funding for incoming visitors

### Takeaway
Dedicated incoming-PhD-visitor schemes are scarce. The main sources are Erasmus+ traineeships funded by the home institution (2026–27 rates of about €540–569/month for Italy and about €480/month for Spain and Portugal), host-specific schemes (BSC mobility, HPC-Europa, Archimedes, Trento's Visiting programme for senior researchers), and the Italian MAECI "Invest Your Talent in Italy" scholarships (master's level).

### Cited Findings
- Erasmus+ traineeship rates for 2026–27: Group 1, which includes Italy, is €569/month (VUB) or €540/month (Utrecht). Group 2, which includes Spain and Portugal, is €480/month (Utrecht, Leiden). The EUI lists €500 for Group 2, which may be an older year. Rates depend on the sending institution, and in Spain SEPIE lets each university set its amount within a range. Durations may be capped, e.g., Utrecht's maximum of 3 months + 22 days for 2026–27 — [VUB 2026-27 PDF](https://www.vub.be/sites/default/files/2026-07/erasmus%2B%20grants%20TRAINEESHIPS%202026%202027.pdf); [Utrecht](https://students.uu.nl/studeren-in-het-buitenland/financiering-en-beurzen/erasmus-grants/erasmus-grants-for-traineeships); [Leiden](https://www.student.universiteitleiden.nl/en/scholarships/sea/erasmus-for-traineeships?cd=biology-msc); [EUI](https://www.eui.eu/en/services/academic-service/international-mobility/erasmus-mobility-programme); [UdG](https://www.udg.edu/ca/internacional/Beques-Grants-Becas/Erasmus-for-Traineeships-English)
- Italy: the MAECI Invest Your Talent (IYT) scholarship regulations are public. It is aimed at master's-level study, not PhD visits — [IYT regulations](https://investyourtalentapplication.esteri.it/SitoIYT/EN/pdf/Regulations_of_the_IYT_Scholaship.pdf)
- Trento: "Visiting in Trentino" from the Autonomous Province of Trento (6–9 months, experienced researchers) — [FBK magazine](https://magazine.fbk.eu/en/news/visiting-in-trentino-2024-call-open-for-applications/)
- Greece: Archimedes (RRF-funded for 4 years from 2022) funds visiting researchers — [Athena RC](https://www.athenarc.gr/en/node/5164)
- Spain: the BSC Mobility Programme covers travel and accommodation for collaborating-institution visitors — [BSC](https://bsc.es/node/30382). "la Caixa" INPhINIT funds full PhDs at BSC, not visits — [BSC INPhINIT](https://bsc.es/ca/printpdf/news/bsc-news/bsc-opens-applications-8-predoctoral-inphinitlacaixa-fellowships)

### Inferences
- The FAIR PNRR cascade calls were aimed at companies and partners, not visiting PhDs.
- With RRF funding winding down (Archimedes' 4 years run to about end-2025), Archimedes' 2026 visitor budget may be uncertain.

### Gaps
- Spanish government incoming-visitor schemes (e.g., the "Ayudas María Zambrano" for senior researchers; Severo Ochoa centre funds), Portuguese FCT schemes for incoming PhD visitors, Greek ELIDEK, and Cyprus RIF were not verified this session.
- EU COST Action Short-Term Scientific Missions (a common route for 1–3 month visits) were not checked.
- Erasmus+ doctoral short-term mobility (5–30 days, a separate rate) was not retrieved.

## 4. Practicalities: rent and cost of living, visas, language

### Takeaway
Milan is the most expensive city: rooms average about €700/month and 1-bedroom flats run €1,500 or more, with one undated source at €3,000. Barcelona, Rome and Madrid rooms cost about €620–650. Lisbon 1-bedroom flats cost about €1,330–1,410. Athens is cheapest among the capitals with data. Non-EU visitors to Italy should go through the "scientific research" hosting-agreement and nulla osta route, starting at least 3 months ahead, and confirm with the host whether a PhD visitor qualifies.

**Visas (Italy)**
- Non-EU researchers sign a hosting agreement with an accredited research body, get a research nulla osta from the Prefecture for stays over 90 days, then apply for a research visa at the embassy and a residence permit after arrival. Stays are short (up to 90 days) or long (91–365 days). Eligibility requires a PhD or a degree that gives access to PhD programmes. Start at least 3 months ahead, and collect the visa within 180 days of the nulla osta — [EU Immigration Portal Italy researcher](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/researcher-italy_en); [UniPi procedures](https://www.unipi.it/en/about-us/international-relations/welcome-services/entry-and-stay-information-for-non-eu-citizens/research-entry-visa-clearance/instructions-and-procedures/); [UniGe >3 months](https://unige.it/en/ricerca/accoglienza-ricercatori-extra-ue-periodi-ricerca-superiori-3-mesi); [UniBo visiting researchers](https://www.unibo.it/en/international/international-teaching-and-research-staff/information-for-international-teaching-and-research-staff-hosted-at-the-university-of-bologna/visiting-researchers); [Sapienza procedure](https://www.uniroma1.it/en/node/465126)
- Caveat: some sources say the research route cannot be used by people entering Italy to obtain a PhD. Visiting PhDs enrolled elsewhere are generally treated as visiting researchers, so confirm with the host — same sources as above.

**Language**
- IMDEA Software's working language is English — [IMDEA careers](https://software.imdea.org/careers/2025-10-intern-distributed/). Research groups at all listed hubs generally work in English [inference]. Everyday life in Barcelona is bilingual Catalan/Spanish, in Bilbao Basque/Spanish.

### Inferences
- Approximate monthly budget for a single visitor renting a room: Milan €1,300–1,600; Barcelona, Madrid and Rome €1,100–1,400; Lisbon €1,100–1,300; Turin, Genoa, Pisa and Trento lower, about €900–1,200; Athens about €800–1,100. These are estimates from the room rents above plus typical living costs and are not sourced. The Erasmus+ traineeship rate (€480–569) covers only part of this.
- Spain, Portugal and Greece implement the same EU Students & Researchers Directive (2016/801), so each offers a researcher or student visa tied to a host agreement [prior knowledge; not verified this session].
