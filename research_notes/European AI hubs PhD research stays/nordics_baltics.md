# Nordic and Baltic AI Hubs for Visiting PhD Research Stays (DK, SE, NO, FI, IS, EE, LV, LT)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Method note: the hosts fcai.fi, aicentre.dk, ku.dk and wasp-sweden.org could not be fetched from this research session (network-blocked). Findings for those come from search-engine snippets of their own pages, not full-page reads. Anything marked "(prior knowledge, unverified)" comes from background knowledge and was not confirmed this session. Do not state it as fact without checking.

## 1. Hubs: city, strengths, flagship groups/PIs

### Takeaway
The region's AI work is concentrated in a few national centres. Finland has FCAI and the new ELLIS Institute Finland (Espoo/Helsinki). Sweden has WASP and the new ELLIS Unit Sweden (KTH, Chalmers, Uppsala, Linköping). Denmark has the Pioneer Centre for AI (Copenhagen) and the ELLIS Unit Denmark, which replaced the Copenhagen unit in 2026. Norway has NorwAI at NTNU and Integreat at UiO/UiT. Aarhus (COBRA/Concordium) is the strongest academic crypto/blockchain group in the region. The Baltics are thinner: Tartu is the main AI node, and I found no ELLIS unit there.

## 2. Formal visiting-PhD programmes (FCAI, Pioneer Centre, WASP, ELLIS)

### Takeaway
None of the major Nordic centres runs an open, funded call for incoming visiting PhD students. In practice the stay is host-driven: find a PI who applies to a local mobility fund (Aalto AScI, HIIT), or come with your own or home-country funding. The ELLIS PhD exchange and WASP stints mainly serve students already in those programmes. P1 has paused individual research internships.

### Cited Findings
- **FCAI**: the dedicated FCAI visit programme I found is the **Visiting Professor Program**, which is for senior researchers, with terms and compensation negotiated individually. I found no FCAI-specific PhD visit grant. The "FCAI researcher visit programme" named in the brief could not be confirmed — [FCAI visiting professors](https://fcai.fi/visiting-professors)
- **Aalto Science Institute (AScI) Visiting Doctoral Researcher**: funded travel and accommodation for visits of one week to two months. Deadlines were at the end of each month except June and July. **One Aalto page says the programme is discontinued from 2026 onwards**, while another still lists it. An Aalto FAQ says a host professor can apply to AScI to fund a visiting PhD student's travel and accommodation for up to two months. Check with asci@aalto.fi — [AScI visiting scientist programmes](https://www.aalto.fi/en/aalto-science-institute-asci/visiting-scientist-programmes); [AScI FAQ](https://aalto.fi/en/aalto-science-institute-asci/faq); [AScI relaunch news](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/asci-visiting-researcher-programme-relaunched)
- **HIIT Event and Mobility Programme** (Helsinki Institute for Information Technology): funds IT researcher visits. The main applicant must be a PI at Aalto or the University of Helsinki, so the host applies. Longer visits get only partial funding — [HIIT funding](https://www.hiit.fi/funding/)
- **Pioneer Centre for AI**: P1 is **not currently accepting individual research-internship applications**. Students are told to ask their home international or faculty office whether a programme agreement with P1 is possible. P1 news does describe past hosted "Visiting Researcher" PhD stays — [P1 graduate programmes and internships](https://www.aicentre.dk/graduate-programmes-and-internships); [P1 graduate studies](https://www.aicentre.dk/graduate-studies/)
- **WASP research stints abroad** are outbound only, for WASP and WASP-affiliated PhD students: 1–6 months, either one long visit (about one semester) or at most two short ones (each at least one month). The application is a plan of up to two pages, a host invitation letter and a CV. WASP also funds self-organised study trips. I found no WASP scheme for incoming non-WASP PhD students — [WASP handbook: research stints abroad](https://internal.wasp-sweden.org/wasp-handbook/graduate-school/research-stints-abroad/); [WASP internationalization](https://wasp-sweden.org/graduate-school/internationalization)
- **ELLIS PhD & Postdoc Program**: the 2027 call closed on 31 Oct 2026 (23:59 AoE). The portal opened on 1 Oct, review runs Nov–Dec, and decisions come in Jan–Feb 2027. It requires at least six months (can be split) with a co-advisor in a different European country. Contact: phd@ellis.eu. A currently enrolled PhD student can't join through the main call. The route is to have an ELLIS member supervisor at home and a Nordic ELLIS co-advisor — [ELLIS call 2026](https://ellis.eu/news/ellis-phd-program-call-for-applications-2026); [ELLIS programme details](https://ellis.eu/research/phd-postdoc/phd-postdoc-program-details); [ELLIS PhD & Postdoc](https://ellis.eu/phd-postdoc)
- **Concordium (Aarhus)**: there is a "visitors" section on the people page, but no formal call was found — [COBRA people and visitors](https://cs.au.dk/research/centers/concordium/people-and-visitors)
- **Swedish Research Council (VR)**: has a grant for recruiting international visiting researchers to Sweden (2026 call announced 24 Nov 2025, aimed at researchers from outside Europe). The host university applies — [VR call](https://www.vr.se/english/applying-for-funding/calls/2025-11-24-grant-for-recruiting-international-visiting-researchers-to-sweden-2026.html); [VR news](https://www.vr.se/english/just-now/news/news-archive/2025-11-24-we-are-announcing-grants-for-recruiting-researchers-from-outside-europe-in-2026-as-well.html)

### Inferences
- The most realistic routes are: (a) your own or home-country funding plus a host invitation letter; (b) the host applies to AScI or HIIT in Helsinki, or uses its own grant; (c) for ELLIS members, an ELLIS exchange with a Nordic co-advisor (ELLIS Institute Finland, Unit Helsinki, Unit Denmark, Unit Sweden).
- With P1 internships paused, Copenhagen visits are best arranged with an individual DIKU, DTU or ITU PI.

### Gaps
- FCAI's 2025–26 visitor web pages could not be read directly. Whether FCAI now runs a PhD-level "researcher visit programme" (stipend, deadlines) is unverified.
- I found no stipend amounts for the ELLIS exchange (funding usually comes from the home supervisor). Unverified.

## 3. Region-specific funding

### Takeaway
Several classic incoming scholarships have **closed**: EDUFI Fellowship (ended 2025) and Estonia's Dora Plus visiting-doctoral grant (ended 2022). The Swedish Institute Visby programme's current status is unclear. What remains is mostly Erasmus+ (for EU-programme PhD students), home-country funders, and host-applied grants.

### Cited Findings
- **EDUFI Fellowship (Finland)**: €1,900/month, with no housing or travel cover. **Ended at the end of 2025. No new applications after 17 Oct 2025.** Aggregator sites advertising "EDUFI 2026" contradict the official notice — [OPH EDUFI Fellowship](https://www.oph.fi/en/internationalisation/edufi-fellowship); [OPH news: ending](https://www.oph.fi/en/news/2025/finnish-national-agency-educations-edufi-fellowship-doctoral-researchers-ending)
- **Dora Plus (Estonia, run by the Education and Youth Board, HARNO)**: the visiting-doctoral grant paid €660/month plus a mobility grant, for stays of 30 days to 10 months. **Activities ended on 31 Dec 2022, with no new calls.** — [UT Dora Plus visiting doctoral](https://ut.ee/en/node/114004); [TLU Dora Plus 2.2](https://www.tlu.ee/en/dora-plus-action-22); [EMU](https://www.emu.ee/en/studies/doctoral-school/dora-/dora-plus-phd-student-mobility/)
- **Kristjan Jaak**: funds outbound mobility for PhD students at Estonian institutions (up to 30 days), through HARNO. It is not for incoming visitors — [HARNO PDF](https://harno.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2024-06/Scholarship%20for%20studies%20abroad%20and%20mobility_HARNO.pdf); [RUG summary](https://www.rug.nl/education/scholarships/kristjan-jaak-scholarship-short-study-visits)
- **UT CEURUS visiting PhD scholarships**: a University of Tartu programme for visiting PhD students in its regional (Eurasian) studies area, not AI — [CEURUS](https://ceurus.ut.ee/home/fellowships/phd-scholarships/)
- **Swedish Institute Visby programme**: I found no official 2026 call on si.se. Aggregators conflict on eligible countries (Eastern Partnership / Russia / Baltics) and describe stays of up to 12 months for PhD students. The SI Scholarship for Global Professionals is for master's study, not PhD visits — [SI Global Professionals](https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals/); [aggregator, unverified](https://o3schools.com/swedish-institute-visby-programme-scholarships-for-phd-studies-postdoctoral-research/)
- **HIIT mobility** and **AScI** (see section 2) are the Helsinki host-applied options. **VR's international visiting researcher grant** (Sweden) is also host-applied — sources as above.

### Inferences
- Erasmus+ traineeship mobility (2–12 months) is probably the most dependable scheme for a PhD student enrolled at an EU or Erasmus+ programme-country university, if the home and host universities have an agreement or traineeship arrangement. This is general programme knowledge and was not verified this session.
- Nordplus targets institutions in Nordic and Baltic countries, so it only helps if the home university is in the region (prior knowledge, unverified).

### Gaps
- Nordplus, Nordic Council schemes, Danish incoming grants and Norwegian incoming grants: not researched in detail. No sources collected.
- Whether HARNO or Estonia has a successor to Dora Plus for incoming doctoral students is unknown.

## 4. Practicalities: rent, visas, language

### Takeaway
Expect monthly rent of roughly €350–900 for a room or studio in Helsinki, SEK 4,500–10,000 in Stockholm, DKK 4,000–10,000 in Copenhagen, and NOK 8,400–18,900 in Oslo. Tartu is cheaper (no data found). EU/EEA citizens need only local registration. From 11 June 2026, Sweden lets non-EU visiting doctoral students get a research residence permit (3–48 months) rather than only a visitor permit.

### Cited Findings
- **Helsinki**: HOAS rooms cost €350–550/month and private rentals €500–900. A studio is €700–900, or over €1,000 in central areas. One market tracker gives an all-types average rent of €869 — [study-abroad.org 2026 guide](https://www.study-abroad.org/blog/fi-accommodation-guide/); [HOAS](https://hoas.fi/en/your-guide-to-student-life-in-finland/student-housing-what-to-expect/affordable-student-housing-in-the-helsinki-area/); [erasmusplay](https://erasmusplay.com/en/market-trends/fi/helsinki); [Aalto housing](https://www.aalto.fi/en/services/housing-for-students)
- **Stockholm**: a corridor room costs SEK 4,500–6,500/month and a studio SEK 7,000–10,000 (student union report, Aug 2026) — [SFS Bostadsrapport 2026](https://sfs.se/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Bostadsrapport-2026.pdf)
- **Copenhagen**: average youth-housing rent is about DKK 8,100 in Copenhagen K and about DKK 6,500 outside the centre (Q2 2025). Another guide gives DKK 4,000–10,000 — [Savills Q2 2025](https://www.savills.com/research_articles/255800/379472-0); [uni2study](https://www.uni2study.com/articles/student-housing-in-europe-by-country)
- **Oslo**: an SiO student studio at Nordberg costs NOK 8,400–8,715/month (from Aug 2026). A central one-bed is about NOK 18,900, or about NOK 14,500 outside the centre — [SiO housing](https://bolig.sio.no/en/housingtype/NORE1U); [uni2study](https://www.uni2study.com/articles/student-housing-in-europe-by-country)
- **Sweden visas**: from 11 June 2026, visiting doctoral students can get a residence permit for research (3–48 months) instead of only a visitor permit (max 12 months). Permanent residence now requires three years of permits instead of four. The researcher maintenance requirement is at least SEK 9,700/month plus the return journey. Stays under three months need no residence permit. Doctoral-student permits require an admission decision, not just a promise — [KI news 11-06-2026](https://news.ki.se/new-migration-regulations-for-researchers-and-doctoral-students); [SLU PDF](https://internt.slu.se/globalassets/mw/stod-serv/hr-fragor/internationella-anstallningar/new-migration-policy-affecting-researchers-and-doctoral-students-as-of-june-2026.pdf); [Migrationsverket researchers](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/work/employee-or-self-employed/researchers.html); [Migrationsverket: doctoral student or researcher](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/study/doctoral-student-or-researcher.html)

### Inferences
- All eight countries are in the EU/EEA and Schengen, so EU citizens only register locally (for example the Finnish DVV or Danish CPR) for stays over three months. Non-EU visitors staying under 90 days need a Schengen visa or visa exemption. Longer stays need a national researcher or student permit, normally backed by a host hosting agreement under the EU Students and Researchers Directive. Denmark (non-EU, Directive opt-out) has its own researcher/guest-researcher permit schemes. This is general knowledge, not verified this session.
- Language: English is the working language in all of these labs (prior knowledge).
