## 1. ELLIS PhD & Postdoc Program, ELLIS mobility funds, Horizon Europe AI networks (ELIAS/ELSA/ELISE/TAILOR/ELLIOT etc.)

### Takeaway
ELLIS is the main structured route. It requires at least 6 months of exchange with a co-advisor in another European country. The 2026 call (for 2027 entry) closed on 31 Oct 2026, and reference letters are due 25 Nov 2026. Students outside ELLIS mostly reach ELLIS labs through project-funded mobility grants: ELISE ran one, and ELIAS now has mobility funds that appear to be tied to specific sites. The Horizon Europe "AI lighthouse" networks from the 2022 call are ending or have been extended (ELSA runs to Aug 2026). ELLIOT (2025–2029) and the new RAISE "AI for science" networks of excellence are the current or successor vehicles.

### Inferences
- A PhD student outside ELLIS cannot simply "join" mid-PhD. The realistic routes are: (a) apply to the ELLIS call in Oct as a current student, which some units accept, so check the guidelines; (b) get a visit funded through an ELLIS unit's or network project's mobility budget (ELIAS/ELLIOT) by asking the host PI directly; (c) pair an ELLIS host with Erasmus+, COST or national funding.
- TAILOR, AI4Media, euROBIN and the AI-on-Demand platform had "connectivity fund" or exchange schemes under H2020 or the early Horizon Europe period. Most of these projects date from 2020–2023, so in late 2026 they are probably ended or finishing [UNVERIFIED / background knowledge]. ELLIOT, the RAISE networks and the GenAI4EU/ADRA calls are where new mobility money is likelier to appear.

## 2. Erasmus+ (doctoral short-term and long-term mobility, traineeships, rates, Austria)

### Takeaway
Doctoral candidates can use Erasmus+ KA131 in two ways: short-term physical mobility of 5–30 days (paid per day), or long-term study or traineeship mobility of 2–12 months (paid per month). There is a 12-month cap per study cycle, including the doctorate. Traineeships can be done at research institutes or companies, not only universities. Monthly rates depend on the sending country and institution, roughly EUR 570–750 per month for "Group 1" destinations in 2026–27. Austrian universities may cap durations for budget reasons; TU Wien currently caps traineeships at 4 months.

### Cited Findings
- Short-term doctoral physical mobility lasts 5–30 days — [Uni Göttingen](https://uni-goettingen.de/en/erasmus%2b+phd+shorttermmobility+-+outgoing/704551.html). It can be blended with an unfunded virtual component of up to 12 months — [Univ. Ljubljana](https://www.fu.uni-lj.si/en/erasmus-short-term-doctoral-study-and-or-traineeship-mobility/)
- **Short-term daily rates.** EUR 79 per day for days 1–14 for all countries (Siegen, short-term doctoral) — [Uni Siegen](https://www.uni-siegen.de/en/erasmus-grants). BTU lists EUR 79 per day for days 1–14 and EUR 56 per day for days 15–30, for blended mobility — [BTU Cottbus](https://www.b-tu.de/en/leonardo/erasmus-funding). PSL adds a top-up of EUR 100 for stays of 14 days or less and EUR 150 for longer — [PSL](https://psl.eu/en/international/study-abroad/doctoral-mobility-erasmus)
- **Long-term traineeships** last 2–12 months excluding travel. Doctoral mobility can include a complementary traineeship within the maximum — [UK gov Erasmus HE guidance, Apr 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-erasmus-programme/erasmus-programme-guidance-for-higher-education-he)

### Inferences
- For a PhD student in Austria, the cleanest 3–6 month route is an Erasmus+ traineeship at a research lab such as an ELLIS unit, Max Planck institute, INRIA or company lab. A learning agreement for traineeships is needed, and no inter-institutional agreement is required for traineeships [UNVERIFIED / background knowledge: standard KA131 rule]. Erasmus+ can usually be combined with national grants such as Marietta Blau or KWA, but each funder's double-funding rules apply.
- At roughly EUR 600–750 per month, Erasmus+ alone will not cover rent in Zurich, London, Paris or Munich. It is a top-up to continued salary or a stipend.

## 3. MSCA (Doctoral Networks, Staff Exchanges) and COST Actions STSMs

### Takeaway
MSCA Staff Exchanges (SE) fund secondments of 1–12 months, including for doctoral candidates, at EUR 2,870 per person-month in the 2026 call. The 2026 deadline was 16 Apr 2026, and a 2027 call page exists. A PhD student only benefits if their group is already a partner in a funded SE or Doctoral Network project. COST STSMs are the most accessible small grant: 5–90 days, up to 180 days for early-career researchers. The grant is typically capped at about EUR 2,000–4,000 depending on the Action. Several AI-related Actions are active.

### Cited Findings
- **MSCA SE 2026** (HORIZON-MSCA-2026-SE-01-01). Opened 16 Dec 2025 and closed 16 Apr 2026, 17:00 Brussels time. Budget EUR 97.9M — [MSCA news](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/whats-new/news/msca-opens-eu979-million-call-for-staff-exchanges); [MSCA SE 2026 call](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/calls/msca-staff-exchanges-2026). The 2027 call page is [MSCA SE 2027](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/funding/msca-staff-exchanges-2027). FFG's Oct 2026 update deck is [FFG PDF](https://www.ffg.at/sites/default/files/2026-10/MSCA_SE_2026_27_BF_Update_Call_27_0.pdf)
- **SE unit cost** is EUR 2,870 per person-month, up from EUR 2,710 in 2025 — [UKRO presentation](https://www.ukro.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Presentation-MSCA-SE-2026-UK-24-02-26.pdf); [FCT PDF](https://www.fct.pt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026_01_MSCA_SE.pdf); [NKFIH slides](https://nkfih.gov.hu/hivatalrol/horizont-europa/eloadasok/borza-beatrix-marie-260204). Payments are pro-rated on 30-day months, and secondments must be full-time — [FFG](https://www.ffg.at/sites/default/files/2026-10/MSCA_SE_2026_27_BF_Update_Call_27_0.pdf)
- Secondments within MSCA Doctoral Networks are part of the DN project design. They are only available to DN-recruited doctoral candidates [UNVERIFIED / background knowledge].
- **COST STSM general rules.** 5–90 days, up to 180 days for early-career investigators. Amounts are set by the Action using duration, destination, cost context and available funds. The COST Annotated Rules (Nov 2025 version) govern — [UCC/STOCHASTICA CA24104](https://www.ucc.ie/en/stochastica/grants/short-termscientificmissions/); [COST networking tools checklist 2026](https://www.cost.eu/uploads/2022/10/Networking-tools-checklist-for-GH-Managers-2026.pdf)

MAIA (AI in Archaeology) caps at EUR 3,000. Its deadline was 31 Jan 2026 and missions had to finish by 15 Sep 2026 — [COST MAIA call](https://www.cost.eu/?p=188287); [ADS MAIA](https://archaeologydataservice.ac.uk/news-events/maia-stsm/)
- **AI-relevant COST Actions** found: CA24136 (Interactions between Control Theory and Machine Learning); newly approved CA25153 (Human-centered Approaches to Responsible Privacy for and with AI) and CA25157 (AI Governance, Use and Impact); CA24104 STOCHASTICA (stochastic modelling); MAIA (AI in archaeology); an ML-for-quantum-chemistry Action (cost-mlqc4fc) — [COST Action list (search result)](https://www.cost.eu/cost-action/page/5/); [cost-mlqc4fc GEC guidelines](https://cost-mlqc4fc.github.io/docs/Grant%20Evaluation%20Committee%20guidelines%20GP1.pdf)
- COST feature on STSM impact — [cost.eu](https://www.cost.eu/short-visits-lasting-impact-2/)

### Inferences
- To use COST, the applicant should join a relevant Action's working group first, which is free and done via e-COST, and then apply in that Action's STSM call. Calls come once or twice per grant period, which runs Nov–Oct.

## 4. National outgoing schemes (Austria focus; Germany, Switzerland and incoming grants briefly)

### Takeaway
For a PhD student at an Austrian university, the main options are the OeAD Marietta Blau grant (6–12 months, about EUR 1,620–1,800 per month, deadlines 1 Feb and 1 Sep). Short stays are covered by university schemes: Uni Wien KWA (2 weeks–3 months) and TU Wien KUWI (1–3 months, EUR 850 per month). Germans use DAAD doctoral annual and short-term grants. Incoming non-EU candidates can use the Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships (CHF 2,450 per month) and DAAD research grants.

## 5. Industry and other funding (fellowships, internships, AI safety funders, compute, HiPEAC)

### Takeaway
Industry PhD fellowships (Google, Apple, Meta) give money, not stays, and are nomination-based or annual with autumn deadlines. ## 6. Application playbook (host choice, PI email, timeline, documents, visa, insurance, social security, housing)

### Takeaway
Start 9–12 months before the stay. Start 12+ months ahead for Marietta Blau or Swiss ESKAS, since their deadlines fall 5–7 months before start, and for industry internships. Secure a host invitation letter first, because almost every grant requires one. Non-EU PhDs can use the EU Researchers Directive (2016/801): a host signs a hosting agreement, and holders of a researcher permit in one EU state can do up to 180 days per 360 in a second EU state without a new permit.

### Cited Findings
- **Directive 2016/801 short-term mobility.** Up to 180 days in any 360-day period in a second Member State, based on a valid researcher permit from the first state. Germany requires no German residence permit for this. Long-term mobility (over 180 days) needs more documents, including a hosting agreement. Ireland and Denmark are not bound by the Directive — [Germany federal portal](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/en/rechte-und-pflichten/102837933); [IND NL short-term mobility](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/short-term-mobility-for-researchers-directive-eu-2016801); [Directive Art. 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2016/801/article/28/data.htm)
- **Hosting agreement** (Art. 10). The research organisation signs it with the third-country researcher. The second state may require its own hosting agreement — [Art. 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2016/801/article/10/data.htm); [Art. 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2016/801/article/28/data.htm)
- The Directive applies to stays over 3 months; stays under 3 months use short-stay visa rules. PhD students can be admitted as paid researchers or as unpaid grant holders — [EURAXESS](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/information); [EU Immigration Portal NL](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/researcher-netherlands_en)
- Example lead times from sources: ELLIS applications in Oct for entry about 10–12 months later. Marietta Blau deadlines on 1 Feb and 1 Sep (6–12 month stays). Swiss ESKAS deadlines in autumn 2026 for a Sep 2027 start. MSCA SE deadline in Apr for projects starting about 9 months later. DeepMind 2027 internships posted Aug–Oct 2026 for May–Jul 2027 (all cited above).

### Inferences / practical guidance [synthesized; largely background knowledge, UNVERIFIED in this session]
- **Choosing a host.** Pick a PI whose last 2–3 years of papers directly overlap your thesis chapter. Check whether they are an ELLIS fellow or scholar, whether the unit has visitor funding (ELLIS units, MPI-IS, ETH AI Center and Inria often host visitors), and whether your supervisor knows them. Warm intros beat cold emails.
- **Cold email contents.** A subject such as "Research visit request: [topic], [dates], self-funded via [grant]". Include: 1 sentence on who you are, your supervisor and institution. 2–3 sentences linking a specific paper of theirs to your concrete planned project. The proposed duration and dates. The funding source: say you are applying for or holding Erasmus+, Marietta Blau or COST, which signals no cost to the host. A request for a short call and an invitation letter. Attach your CV and a 1-page project sketch. Ideally your supervisor sends the email or is CC'd.
- **Documents usually needed.** Research proposal or work plan (2–5 pages, with timeline), CV with publications, host invitation letter (dates, desk, supervision, sometimes "no bench fee"), home supervisor letter or approval, transcripts or doctoral agreement (Marietta Blau needs approved thesis status), Erasmus+ learning agreement for traineeships, budget (Marietta Blau, COST), and a final report afterwards (KUWI: 1–2 pages within 3 months; COST: a scientific report).
- **Insurance.** Within the EU/EEA/CH, carry an EHIC. Add private top-up covering liability and repatriation; Erasmus+ traineeships require accident and liability cover, sometimes arranged by the home university. For the UK, check GHIC/EHIC reciprocity.
- **Social security and tax.** If you stay employed by the Austrian university and are posted temporarily, request an A1 portable document (ÖGK in Austria) to stay in the Austrian system for up to 24 months. Scholarship holders without employment usually don't need an A1 but keep EHIC coverage. Tax residency normally stays at home for stays under 183 days. Not verified this session; the search did not return A1 sources.
- **Housing.** Book early. Use the host university's guest-housing office, EURAXESS service centres, the ETH/EPFL and MPG guest houses, and sublets via lab Slack or Discord. Budget Zurich, London and Paris at EUR 1,200–2,000+ per month for a room.
