# Central and Eastern Europe AI hubs for visiting PhD research stays (3–12 months)

Notes written 24 Nov 2026. Research limits: WebFetch could not reach nawa.gov.pl or insait.ai, because those hosts were blocked in this session. Items marked [UNVERIFIED – background knowledge] are from the researcher's prior knowledge and were not confirmed by a source in this session. Confirm them before relying on them.

## Q1. Hubs by country: city, strengths, flagship groups/PIs

### Takeaway
The region's strongest destinations for agentic/multi-agent work, LLMs and trustworthy AI are:
- **Prague:** CTU AI Center / CIIRC, an ELLIS unit; it signed a 2026 Cisco agentic-AI agreement.
- **Warsaw:** the IDEAS Research Institute, which hosts the ELLIS unit, plus the University of Warsaw (MIM).
- **Kraków:** GMUM at Jagiellonian.
- **Sofia:** INSAIT, the best-funded institute in the region.
- **Bratislava:** KInIT (LLM reliability, interpretability, agentic safety).
- **Ljubljana:** the JSI AI Department / IRCAI.

Hungary's flagship MILAB programme has concluded. IDEAS NCBR as a company no longer exists. Since 2025 its scientific mission belongs to the separate state "IDEAS Research Institute".

### Cited Findings

**Poland — IDEAS NCBR → IDEAS Research Institute (Warsaw)**
- IDEAS NCBR sp. z o.o. merged into Akces NCBR sp. z o.o. on 12 Dec 2025 and ceased operating. Its notice says "IDEAS Instytut Badawczy" has no connection to IDEAS NCBR's activities — [IDEAS NCBR homepage](https://ideas-ncbr.pl/en/); [pl.wikipedia IDEAS NCBR](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDEAS_NCBR)

## Q2. Formal visiting PhD programmes (INSAIT, CIIRC, IDEAS, ELLIS)

### Takeaway
No searched institute has a clearly documented, open "visiting PhD" call with a stipend:
- **ELLIS PhD exchange:** the only structured route. It requires being an ELLIS PhD student, with a 6-month minimum abroad in a different country.
- **INSAIT:** its Graduate Research Fellowship (6–12 months) targets Master's students or graduates, not visiting PhDs.
- **CIIRC, IDEAS, KInIT and JSI:** visits run case by case, through a host PI plus external funding.

### Cited Findings
- **ELLIS PhD & Postdoc Program:**
  - Students must spend at least 6 months with an ELLIS co-advisor in a different European country. The split of that time is flexible.
  - Admission runs as an annual central call.
  - Sources: [ELLIS programme details](https://ellis.eu/research/phd-postdoc/phd-postdoc-program-details); [ELLIS PhD page](https://ellis.eu/phd-postdoc); [admission guidelines](https://ellis.eu/research/phd-postdoc/guidelines-phd-admission-process)
  - Regional ELLIS units: Czechia/Prague [ellis.eu/units/prague](https://ellis.eu/units/prague) and Warsaw [ellis.eu/units/warsaw](https://ellis.eu/units/warsaw). No Budapest ELLIS unit appeared in results.
- **INSAIT Graduate Research Fellowship (GRF):**
  - On-site in Sofia, mostly listed as 6–12 months, though some listings say 8–12 weeks.
  - For Master's students or recent Master's graduates in CS/EE/statistics/mathematics.
  - Pay conflicts: €3,307 gross/month in one listing, €2,000/month in another. Rolling deadline.
  - Pitched as preparation for top PhD programmes.
  - Sources (all aggregators; not confirmed on insait.ai): [aijobs.app](https://aijobs.app/insait-graduate-research-fellowship-program-insait-institute-for-computer--7339133); [opportunitydesk Jan 2026](https://opportunitydesk.org/2026/01/06/insait-graduate-research-fellowship-2026/); [opportunitiescircle](https://www.opportunitiescircle.com/insait-graduate-research-fellowship-in-bulgaria/)
- **INSAIT PhD (full degree, not a visit):** €39,684 gross/year, rolling admission, up to 3 months at a mentor's institution — [insait.ai/phd](https://insait.ai/phd/)
- **CIIRC / CTU:** search found no CIIRC visiting-researcher programme page — [AIC](https://cs.fel.cvut.cz/en/aic)
- **KInIT:** no visiting-researcher scheme found. The PhD programme mentions cooperation with "research units from abroad" — [KInIT PhD](https://kinit.sk/research/phd-study/)
- **Hungary:** the only visitor scheme found is an outdated 2022 call from the Centre for Social Sciences (TK) MILAB, "Visiting Professorship in AI for Social Sciences". It offered HUF 1M per project, a minimum of 2 weeks, and was open to senior researchers and postdocs — [TK MILAB PDF](https://milab.tk.hun-ren.hu/uploads/files/visiting_prof_milab_2022(1).pdf)

### Inferences
- A visiting PhD who is not an ELLIS student should: (1) secure an invitation letter from a PI at CTU, KInIT, IDEAS, INSAIT, MIM or JSI; (2) fund the stay through national mobility schemes (Q3), Erasmus+ traineeship, or home-institution funds.
- INSAIT may accept a PhD student through the GRF channel or as a research intern by arrangement, but this is unconfirmed.

## Q3. Region-specific funding for incoming visitors

### Takeaway
Structured incoming money exists, but mostly for postdocs or by country of origin:
- **Poland:** NAWA's Ulam (PhD holders only; PLN 17k/month in 2026).
- **Slovakia:** SAIA NSP is the best fit for PhD students, at €1,025.50/month, with deadlines 30 Apr and 31 Oct.
- **Visegrad Fund:** short fellowships of 2–10 weeks at €500/week.
- **CEEPUS:** short PhD mobility of up to 12 months.
- **Czech MEYS:** bilateral scholarships, country-specific.
- **Hungary:** no short-stay incoming PhD scheme was found.

### Cited Findings
- **NAWA Ulam (Poland), 2026 call:**
  - For foreign researchers holding a doctorate.
  - Must be employed at a foreign institution, or have been since 31 Aug 2025. This is waived if the doctorate was obtained after 31 Aug 2025.
  - Stay of 6–24 months; pay up to PLN 17,000/month including employer costs; maximum budget PLN 436,800.
  - Applications are submitted by the Polish host institution in the 2026 edition. Results were due by 30 Nov 2026; projects start 1 Feb–1 Dec 2027.
  - Not for current PhD students.
  - Sources: [NAWA Ulam 2026 call](https://nawa.gov.pl/en/nawa/news/call-for-proposals-ulam-nawa-2026); [Call PDF](https://www.nawa.gov.pl/images/Ulam/Ulam_2026/Call-for-applications-Ulam_NAWA_2026.pdf); [fundsforngos](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/education/submissions-open-for-ulam-nawa-programme-poland/)
  - An older edition paid PLN 11,500/month — [gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/greece/call-for-the-7th-edition-of-the-ulam-nawa-program-is-underway)
- **Other NAWA:**
  - Preludium Bis 3 (call to 10 Dec 2026) funds PhD students *in Poland*. It does not fund incoming visitors — [NCBJ](https://www.ncbj.gov.pl/en/information-about-open-calls-projects)
  - The Banach scholarship programme is for students from selected countries — [NAWA Banach](https://www.nawa.gov.pl/en/students/foreign-students/the-banach-scholarship-programme/call-for-proposals)
  - STER (doctoral school internationalisation, institutional) can fund incoming PhD visits through Polish doctoral schools — [NAWA STER 2026](https://nawa.gov.pl/en/nawa/news/ster-nawa-2026-internationalisation-of-doctoral-schools)
- **SAIA National Scholarship Programme (Slovakia):**
  - For PhD students: €1,025.50/month in 2025/26, for 1–10 months [duration UNVERIFIED].
  - Deadlines are 30 Apr (next academic year) and 31 Oct (summer semester), at 16:00 CET. Apply online at scholarships.sk; decisions take about 8 weeks.
  - The next realistic deadline is about 30 Apr 2027. This is inferred, because the 31 Oct 2026 deadline has passed.
  - Covers KInIT, Comenius University and the Slovak University of Technology (STU).
  - Sources: [EURAXESS](https://www.euraxess.es/jobs/funding/national-scholarship-programme-slovak-republic); [minedu.sk](https://www.minedu.sk/national-scholarship-programme-of-the-slovak-republic/); [UZH](https://www.global.uzh.ch/en/global_university/funding-instruments/open_calls/scholarship-program-slovak-republic.html)
- **Visegrad Fund:**
  - Visegrad Fellowship: in-person V4 research stays of 2–10 weeks, at a €500/week lump sum; the 2026 call ran until 31 May 2026.
  - Visegrad Scholarship Program: €3,500/semester; the next call opens 1 Jan 2027.
  - Western Balkans–Visegrad Fellowships run Sep 2026–Jun 2027. Visegrad–Taiwan Scholarships are for PhD candidates.
  - Sources: [Visegrad scholarships](https://www.visegradfund.org/scholarships); [Fellowship](https://www.visegradfund.org/visegrad-fellowship-program-apply); [Scholarship Program](https://www.visegradfund.org/visegrad-scholarship-program-apply); [WB–V4](https://www.visegradfund.org/western-balkans-visegrad-fellowships-apply)
- **Czechia:**
  - The Czech Government scholarship is "solely to promote studies" for 13 developing countries; the 2026 deadline was 30 Sep 2026.
  - MEYS bilateral scholarships (2026/27) include research stays at public universities of up to 10 months. Eligibility depends on country and requires an invitation letter.
  - AKTION (Austria–Czechia) and CEEPUS (PhD short stays of up to 12 months) are run by DZS.
  - Sources: [MEYS 2026/27](https://msmt.gov.cz/en/scholarships/scholarships-for-the-academic-year-2026-27); [FAQ 2026](https://msmt.gov.cz/media/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FAQ_2026-1.pdf); [Charles Univ list](https://cuni.cz/UKEN-2497-version1.pdf); [DZS AKTION](https://www.dzs.cz/en/program/aktion-austria-czech-republic/foreign-exchange-stays)
- **Hungary:** no Hungarian government short-stay scheme for incoming PhD researchers was found. MILAB visitor funding was a 2022 call — [TK MILAB PDF](https://milab.tk.hun-ren.hu/uploads/files/visiting_prof_milab_2022(1).pdf)

### Inferences
- For a 3–12 month PhD stay:
  - **Bratislava (SAIA NSP):** the most bankable funding.
  - **Prague:** Erasmus+ (traineeship or doctoral mobility) or CEEPUS, if the home country is a member.
  - **Poland:** host-side NAWA STER or doctoral-school budgets.
  - **Sofia:** INSAIT's own payroll, if they hire you as a fellow or intern.
- Ulam suits only someone who already holds a PhD.

## Q4. Practicalities: rent, visas, language

### Takeaway
Rents for a one-bedroom are roughly:

| City | Rent (one-bedroom) |
|---|---|
| Sofia | ~€900 average rent (Eurostat-based) |
| Kraków | ~€680 |
| Warsaw | above Prague and Bratislava |
| Prague | ~CZK 20,000+ (~€800) in good districts |
| Ljubljana | ~€1,100 average |

The sources are not like-for-like. EU/EEA visitors need no visa. Non-EU researchers generally use national research or long-stay visas, or the EU Directive 2016/801 researcher permit; details were not verified. English is the working language at all the institutes covered.

### Cited Findings
- **Poland:** the national average one-bedroom rent is about PLN 2,850 (~€660), with central Warsaw at the top — [investropa](https://investropa.com/blogs/news/average-rent-poland). Warsaw one-bedroom rents are higher than Prague, Vienna and Bratislava (Cushman & Wakefield via [property-forum](https://www.property-forum.eu/news/poland-sees-double-digit-rental-growth-over-five-years/18948))
- **Kraków:** ~€680/month for a one-bedroom (date unclear) — [Global Property Guide](https://www.globalpropertyguide.com/europe/poland/rental-yields)
- **Prague:** a one-bedroom in a good district is about CZK 20,000 or more. The Deloitte Q1 2026 average was CZK 466/m² — [expats.cz 2026](https://www.expats.cz/czech-news/article/what-you-ll-pay-for-a-studio-one-bedroom-or-family-apartment-in-2026); [expats.cz](https://www.expats.cz/czech-news/article/prague-rents-hit-a-plateau-but-smaller-flats-are-still-getting-pricier)
- **Budapest:** inner-district one-bedroom about HUF 330,000, outer from HUF 120,000. The same (older) piece gives Bratislava at HUF 235,000 and Ljubljana at HUF 221,000 — [BBJ](https://www.bbj.hu/business/real-estate/residential/bp-rents-cheaper-than-peers-but-not-if-avg-wage-factored-in)
- **Eurostat-based averages (Jun 2026):** Sofia €900 and Ljubljana €1,100. Unit size is not specified — [Euronews Jun 2026](https://www.euronews.com/2026/06/22/average-rents-across-european-capitals-eurostat-reveals-the-most-expensive-countries)
- Bucharest was ranked the most affordable Eastern European capital for renters (2024) — [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/business/bucharest-most-affordable-eastern-europe-capital-renters-2024)

### Inferences
- **SAIA stipend (€1,025/month) vs Bratislava rent:** the stipend roughly covers a shared flat or dorm plus living costs, but not a private one-bedroom.
- **INSAIT GRF (€3,307 gross) vs Sofia:** comfortable.
- **Ulam (PLN 17k including employer costs) vs Warsaw:** comfortable.
