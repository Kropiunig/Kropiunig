## 1. Hubs: city, strengths, flagship groups/PIs

### Takeaway
France has the deepest bench. Inria's national network of centres sits alongside the 2024 "Cluster IA" institutes (PR[AI]RIE-PSAI, Hi! PARIS, SCAI, DATAIA, MIAI, ANITI, 3IA Côte d'Azur) and a strong Paris industry scene (FAIR, GDM, Mistral, Kyutai, HF). The Netherlands is the best ecosystem for multi-agent and agent systems: Delft, Utrecht, Groningen and UvA-ILLC all have strong groups, and UvA/AMLab, CWI and ICAI are also there. For AI x crypto/security, KU Leuven COSIC, CWI Cryptology, UCLouvain Crypto Group, Radboud Digital Security, Inria/ENS crypto teams and Luxembourg SnT (FINATRAX, APSIA) are the standouts.

## 2. Formal visiting PhD programmes (duration, money, deadlines, eligibility)

### Takeaway
Few hubs run open "visiting PhD" calls. The standard route is to email a PI, agree a stay, and get a hosting agreement or visiting status from the institution. Funding comes from your home side, ELLIS/ELISE, Erasmus+ or national schemes. ELLIS is the main structured programme, but it is a full PhD programme with a ≥6-month exchange, not a short-visit scheme, and its 2027 call closed on 31 Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **ELLIS PhD & Postdoc Program**: the academic track requires a visit of at least 6 months to a co-advisor in another European country. The call for 2027 entry closed 31 Oct 2026 (23:59 AoE). G-Research funds scholarships for students with primary advisors at the ELLIS sites in Paris, Tübingen, Munich and Amsterdam from 2026 to 2028 — [ellis.eu/phd-postdoc](https://ellis.eu/phd-postdoc); [2026 call](https://ellis.eu/news/ellis-phd-program-call-for-applications-2026); [G-Research](https://ellis.eu/news/g-research-invests-in-ellis-phd-program-to-support-the-next-generation-of-european-ai-talent)
- **ELLIS Amsterdam**: exchanges are typically funded by the host institution plus ELISE mobility grants — [ELLIS Amsterdam](https://ivi.fnwi.uva.nl/ellis/?p=361)
- **Inria**: no published standalone "visiting PhD programme" was found. Associate Team budgets cover travel and short/medium stays for students but not internship allowances. Team leaders can request separate "Student Mobilities" support from Inria's international department to pay allowances for research visits — [Inria Associate Teams 2026 call](https://www.inria.cl/sites/default/files/2025-08/Associate%20Teams%202026%20Call.pdf); [2027 call](https://www.inria.cl/sites/default/files/2026-07/Appel%20E%CC%81quipes%20Associe%CC%81es%202027-%20EN_0.pdf)

### Inferences
- In practice: secure a PI's agreement first, then a hosting agreement, then funding. Stays of 6 months or more fit ELLIS-style exchanges. For stays under 3 months, a Schengen visa or visitor status is often enough (see Section 4).
- If the requester is in an ELLIS-affiliated lab, an ELLIS/ELISE-style exchange to Paris or Amsterdam is the most "structured" option.

## 3. Region-specific funding

### Takeaway
Incoming PhD funding is thin. Eiffel is the main French government scholarship (€2,100/month at PhD level), but it targets co-supervised PhDs and runs on institutional deadlines around Nov 2026–Jan 2027. FWO's incoming grant is for postdocs. FNR INTER Mobility explicitly excludes PhD candidates. NWO has no general incoming-PhD visit grant. Erasmus+ traineeship (about €540–569/month) is the most universally usable source for students enrolled at an EU/Erasmus+ university.

### Cited Findings
- **France Excellence Eiffel 2026/27 cycle**: €2,100/month at PhD level and €1,200 at Master level, from Jan 2026. The PhD strand runs 12–36 months and can be split into stays of at least 6 months. Tuition is not covered. First-time applicants only, it cannot be combined with another French government grant, and applicants already in France are ineligible. The age cap is reportedly 35. Institution-internal deadlines for the 2027 cycle fall around 6–26 Nov 2026, and the institutions submit to Campus France around 6 Jan 2027. Sources conflict on exact dates, so verify with the host — [Paris-Saclay Eiffel](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en/admission/bourses-et-aides-financieres/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship); [Sorbonne](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/en/news/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-programme-2026/2027); [EURAXESS](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/worldwide/lac/news/france-eiffel-scholarships-phd-and-masters-degrees-2026); [UT3 information booklet](https://www.univ-tlse3.fr/medias/fichier/livret-information-bourses-france-excellence-eiffel-2026-en_1759505006509-pdf). Eiffel's doctoral strand is designed for co-supervision/co-tutelle with a French institution [PK], so it fits poorly for a short 3–6-month visit.
- **Chateaubriand Fellowship**: US-based PhD students only (run by the French Embassy in the US) [PK]. Not relevant unless the requester is enrolled in the US.
- **Campus France bilateral / embassy grants (e.g. "Hubert Curien" partnerships, country-specific doctoral mobility grants)**: these vary by home country [PK]. Check the Campus France office in the requester's country.
- **FWO "Grant for a scientific stay in Flanders"**: for postdoctoral researchers at non-Belgian institutions, covering 1–3-month stays. The amount could not be confirmed because fwo.be was not reachable here — [FWO page](https://fwo.be/en/support-programmes/all-calls/mobility/grant-for-a-scientific-stay-in-flanders/); [UHasselt summary](https://www.uhasselt.be/en/calls-research/detail/1120-fwo-grant-for-a-scientific-stay-in-flanders). For comparison, FWO's outgoing short-visit rate is €66/day for accommodation, capped at €1,650 — [FWO regs](https://fwo.be/en/support-programmes/regulations/regulations-for-grant-for-a-short-study-visit-abroad/)
- **University BOF incoming mobility (Flanders)**: the UHasselt example pays up to €1,650/month plus €500 (Europe) or €1,000 (non-Europe) for travel. It is applied for by the host — [UHasselt BOF](https://www.uhasselt.be/en/calls-research/detail/1337-2023-10-bof-incoming-mobility). VUB runs international research mobility grants — [VUB](https://www.vub.be/en/our-research/become-researcher-vub/research-funding/mobility-funding). KU Leuven has comparable internal funds [PK].
- **FNR INTER Mobility (Luxembourg)**: does NOT fund stays of PhD candidates. The incoming strand is for senior R3–R4 researchers — [FNR INTER Mobility](https://www.fnr.lu/funding-instruments/inter-mobility/)
- **Erasmus+ traineeship 2026–27**: €540/month for "Group 1" host countries (BE, FR, LU and others) per Utrecht and Leiden, and €569/month at VUB (FR, NL, LU in Group 1) plus a €250 top-up for some students. Funded months are capped per institution (Utrecht about 3 months 22 days; Leiden 60 days, with the rest as zero-grant). Up to 12 months per study cycle, PhD included. The host must be in a different country from the home institution — [UU](https://students.uu.nl/en/study-abroad/funding-and-grants/erasmus/erasmus-grants-for-traineeships); [Leiden](https://www.student.universiteitleiden.nl/en/scholarships/sea/erasmus-for-traineeships); [VUB PDF](https://www.vub.be/sites/default/files/2026-07/erasmus%2B%20grants%20TRAINEESHIPS%202026%202027.pdf); [UvA](https://student.uva.nl/en/information/erasmus-traineeship-programme-and-grant)
- **NWO**: no incoming-PhD visit instrument was found in this session [PK: NWO funds Dutch-based PIs; visitors are usually funded from a host's grant].
- **Paris Region**: [PK] the "Paris Region Fellowship Programme" (via DIM / Paris Region) has targeted postdocs, not PhD visitors. Not verified for 2026.

### Inferences
- The realistic funding mix for a non-French, non-Belgian PhD is: home-university or travel grant + Erasmus+ traineeship (if eligible) + a host-PI top-up or internal mobility fund + an industry internship salary if going to a lab.
- Eiffel only makes sense for a long co-supervised arrangement.

## 4. Practicalities: cost of living, housing, visas, language

### Takeaway
Amsterdam is the most expensive and hardest housing market (rooms about €950/month). Paris rooms run about €800+ and Leuven about €600–800 all-in. Delft is cheaper on paper (about €480) but scarce. Luxembourg and Grenoble figures were not found here. Non-EU visitors staying more than 90 days need a hosting agreement (EU Directive 2016/801) everywhere in the region. In France the funding level decides between "passeport talent – chercheur" and a student visa. In NL the host must be an IND-recognised sponsor and meet an income threshold of about €1,767/month.

### Cited Findings
**Rent**
- Amsterdam: average student room about €950/month in Q2 2026 (Kamernet), the most expensive Dutch city; Netherlands national average about €660 (Q1 2026) — [HousingAnywhere/Kamernet Q2 2026](https://housinganywhere.com/rent-index-by-city-q2-2026); [Kamernet Q1 2026](https://housinganywhere.com/kamernet-rent-report-q1-2026); earlier ~€1,000 — [DutchReview](https://dutchreview.com/news/student-room-amsterdam-almost-1000-per-month-2025/)
- Paris: room rents down 4.0% year on year (Q2 2026, HousingAnywhere). Absolute figures conflict between sources (about €800 for a room vs about €1,300 for a studio-type measure) — [HousingAnywhere Q2 2026](https://housinganywhere.com/rent-index-by-city-q2-2026)
- Delft: private room average about €480 (Kamernet), the cheapest in the Randstad. The date is unclear and may be older — [Delta TU Delft](https://delta.tudelft.nl/?p=174631)
- Leuven: KU Leuven gives €490–800/month incl. costs for private rooms and €400–1,000 in KU Leuven residences (AY 25–26). Erasmus Play median is €760 (July 2026). Listings for 2026–27 run about €600–750 + €75–125 charges. VRT reported the Leuven kot market "cooling" in March 2026 — [KU Leuven housing](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/life-at-ku-leuven/housing/students); [Erasmus Play](https://erasmusplay.com/en/market-trends/be/leuven); [VRT](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/03/17/kotenmarkt-leuven-koelt-af/)
- Brussels room rents fell 7.7% year on year (Q2 2026) — [HousingAnywhere](https://housinganywhere.com/rent-index-by-city-q2-2026)
- Grenoble [PK]: about €450–600 for a room/studio. Luxembourg [PK]: about €900–1,300 for a room, among the highest in Europe. Neither was verified.

**Visas, France**
- A hosting agreement (convention d'accueil, Cerfa 16079*03) is required for a Passeport Talent – Chercheur. The total funding amount decides whether a visiting PhD gets passeport talent or a student visa. A 2025 decree reintroduced a minimum funding threshold, with a reference point around the net doctoral-contract salary (about €1,768/month in one university's guidance). The VLS-TS must be validated online within 3 months. **Visas, Netherlands**
- Researcher residence permit under Directive (EU) 2016/801 requires an employment contract or hosting agreement with a recognised research organisation, stating the aim, duration and funds — [IND](https://ind.nl/en/work/pages/scientific-researcher.aspx)
- Income threshold: €1,767/month for 1 Jul 2026–1 Jan 2027 (Wageningen) and €1,734.57 from 1 Jan 2026 (VU). It is adjusted twice a year — [WUR](https://www.wur.nl/en/education/phd-programme/visa-and-residence-permit); [VU](https://vu.nl/en/employee/internationalisation/international-staff)

**Visas, Belgium**
- Only an approved research organisation can sign a hosting agreement, and only by a person empowered to bind it legally. In Flanders the Flemish model hosting agreement must be used, with a decision within 60 days of admissibility — [BELSPO](https://belspo.be/Belspo/visa/conv_en.stm); [Vlaanderen](https://www.vlaanderen.be/en/working-enterprise-and-investment/working/employing-a-foreigner-in-flanders/categories/researcher-with-a-hosting-agreement)

**Visas, general**
- Stays under 90 days are usually possible on a Schengen short-stay visa or visa-free entry, depending on nationality [PK]. Directive 2016/801 also allows intra-EU short-term mobility of up to 180 days in any 360 for someone holding a researcher permit from another EU state, via notification [PK].
- Luxembourg uses the same Directive 2016/801 hosting-agreement route for researchers [PK].

**Language**
- [PK] English is the working language in essentially all listed AI labs. Daily life in NL and Flanders is easy in English. Paris, Grenoble and Toulouse admin and housing are mostly in French. Luxembourg is trilingual (Luxembourgish/French/German), with English widely used.

### Inferences
- Book Amsterdam housing as early as possible or use university guest housing. Delft and Leiden are cheaper alternatives to commute from within the Randstad.
- A non-EU visiting PhD with less than about €1,770/month in funding may fail the French talent-chercheur threshold and the Dutch IND income test. Funding level directly affects visa feasibility.
