# DACH AI hubs (Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Liechtenstein) for visiting PhD research stays (3–12 months)

Research date: 24 Nov 2026. Method note: WebFetch was blocked in this session for the primary domains (ellis.eu, www2.daad.de, phd.ista.ac.at returned DNS failure: they are not on the session's network allowlist), so every finding below comes from search-result snippets of the official pages or aggregators, not full-page reads. Treat amounts and deadlines as needing a final check on the linked page. Items marked "[background, unverified]" are from prior knowledge and were NOT confirmed by a 2026 source. Only the Gaps sections contain them.

## Q1. Hubs: city, strengths, flagship groups/PIs, reputation signals

### Takeaway
The densest cluster for the requester's interests (LLM agents, multi-agent safety, evaluation, trustworthy AI, security) is Tübingen (ELLIS Institute + MPI-IS + Tübingen AI Center), followed by Saarbrücken (CISPA, MPI-SWS, MPI-INF), Zurich (ETH AI Center), Lausanne (EPFL), Munich (MCML/TUM/LMU) and Vienna/Klosterneuburg/Linz (ISTA, TU Wien, JKU). Only Tübingen was verified at PI level in this session.

## Q2. Formal visiting-PhD / research-stay programs

### Takeaway
Few DACH institutions run open calls for visiting PhD students. The main structured routes are ISTA's year-round Scientific Internships (2–12 months, group-decided), the ELLIS PhD exchange (only for ELLIS PhD students; at least 6 months), MPI/CS@Max Planck internships (12–14 weeks, aimed mostly at students before a PhD), and industry PhD internships. CISPA's internships exclude PhD students.

### Cited Findings
- **ISTA (Klosterneuburg near Vienna, AT): Scientific Internships (year-round).** Research groups host visiting PhD candidates from other institutions for 2–12 months, decided case by case. If a group selects you, it sets the next steps and conditions. Students with external scholarships (e.g. Erasmus+) can join for 2–6 months. Visiting students are not employees and get no salary. Joining does not improve admission chances to the ISTA PhD program — [ISTA Scientific Internships](https://phd.ista.ac.at/scientific-internships/)
- ISTA ISTernship (summer): it includes a travel allowance and OeAD-assisted health insurance. Students already accepted to or enrolled in a PhD are not eligible — [ISTernship](https://phd.pages.ist.ac.at/isternship/)
- ISTA also has a separate "scientific visitors with PhD" route (postdoc-level) and a residencies program — [ISTA visitors with PhD](https://postdoc.pages.ist.ac.at/scientific-visitors-with-phd/); [ISTA residencies](https://ista.ac.at/en/institute/residencies/)
- **ELLIS PhD & Postdoc Program exchange:** the stay must last at least 6 months at a top academic institution abroad, or at least 6 months at a European industry lab for the industry track. Funding normally comes from the advisors, and ELLIS itself funds only small travel grants — [Cambridge ELLIS unit page](https://www.ellis.eng.cam.ac.uk/?p=5527); [ELLIS PhD info for applicants 2025 (PDF)](https://preview.ellis.eu/assets/documents/ellis-phd-&-postdoc-program-info-for-applicants-2025.pdf)
- The ELISE Mobility Program paid up to EUR 2,500 travel per researcher but ran only 1 Sep 2020 – 31 Aug 2023, so it has ENDED. The program now continues under the Horizon Europe project ELIAS (2024 announcement) — [ELISE T&C (PDF)](https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/5f55e90f6a7294f66f94d30d/61ea82242923767d20c3e03c_Terms%20and%20Conditions%20of%20the%20ELISE%20Mobility%20Program%20for%20PhDs%20and%20Postdocs.pdf); [ELLIS mobility programs](https://ellis.eu/mobility-programs)
- An ELLIS mobility page mentions scholarships for the mandatory stay of ELLIS PhD students in Germany: a EUR 1,300/month base stipend plus a relocation package (date not confirmed) — [ELLIS mobility fund](https://ellis.eu/mobility-fund)
- **June 2026:** G-Research funds 24 ELLIS PhD students doing their 6-month exchange visit, covering fees and associated expenses. Contact: phd@ellis.eu — [G-Research invests in ELLIS PhD program](https://ellis.eu/news/g-research-invests-in-ellis-phd-program-to-support-the-next-generation-of-european-ai-talent)
- **CS@Max Planck / MPI-INF, MPI-SWS, MPI-SP research internships:** applications go through an online portal, in all CS areas. One summary describes a fully funded program of 12–14 weeks, with a deadline listed as 1 Nov 2026 for summer 2027 (aggregator; verify) — [CIS internships](https://cis.mpg.de/internships); [MPI-SWS internship (aggregator)](https://www.heysuccess.com/opportunity/Max-Planck-Institute-for-Software-Systems-(MPI-SWS)-Internship-Program-(2028)-6136)
- MPI-SWS doctoral admissions run year-round, with decisions 4 times a year. CS@Max Planck has a deadline around 31 Dec for the following fall. These are full PhDs — [MPI-SWS graduate studies](https://mpi-sws.org/graduate-studies/); [MPI-INF PhD applications](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/de/departments/computer-vision-and-machine-learning/application/phd-applications)
- IMPRS-IS: existing PhD students can apply only within the first year of their doctorate — [IMPRS-IS application](https://imprs.is.mpg.de/application)
- **CISPA:** the Summer Research / Young Researcher Internship programs are for BSc/MSc students, and PhD students are explicitly not eligible. One CRISTAL lab posting does list "Research Interns (BSc/MSc/PhD)", which conflicts with that — [CISPA YRIP](https://career.cispa.de/yrip.html); [CISPA internship](https://career.cispa.de/internship); [CISPA FAQ](https://career.cispa.de/faq)
- Some CISPA PhD ads list rolling review until 15 Dec 2026 — [CISPA general PhD call](https://career.cispa.de/jobs/general-phd-call-summer-2026-130)
- **DAAD Postdoc-NeT-AI short-term research stay** (1–3 months; EUR 2,800/month for holders of a PhD; deadline 30 Nov 2026). This is for postdocs, not PhD students — [DAAD Postdoc-NeT-AI (PDF)](https://static.daad.de/media/daad_de/der-daad/postdocnet/26_11_short-term_research_stay-postdocnet-ai.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a PhD student who is not in the ELLIS program, the realistic formal programs are ISTA Scientific Internships and industry internships. Everywhere else, a PI must agree to host and the funding comes from an external grant (DAAD, SGES, Ernst Mach, home institution, Erasmus+).
- Enrolling in the ELLIS PhD program is the best way to get a structured, possibly G-Research-funded 6-month exchange, but you must apply to it early in your PhD.

### Gaps
- No confirmed ETH AI Center, EPFL, Tübingen AI Center, MCML, BIFOLD, DFKI, hessian.AI, JKU, TU Wien or Helmholtz AI visiting-PhD program. [background, unverified]: DFKI and hessian.AI host guest researchers informally, Helmholtz AI has historically run "Helmholtz Visiting Researcher Grants", and Idiap/IDSIA host interns case by case. These are not confirmed for 2026.

- Industry labs other than Google (Aleph Alpha, Bosch Center for AI, Amazon Tübingen, Meta, Microsoft Research) were not verified.

## Q3. Informal route: approaching PIs directly

### Takeaway
At ISTA and most DACH groups the host PI decides. The standard approach is a concise email with a project proposal, CV, a recommendation from your advisor and a named funding source. Some labs (ETH Agentic Systems Lab) require their form and ignore cold emails.

### Cited Findings
- ISTA: individual groups decide whether to host and set the conditions — [ISTA Scientific Internships](https://phd.ista.ac.at/scientific-internships/)
- ETH Agentic Systems Lab: apply through the form plus interview, and proposing your own project is strongly encouraged. Inquiries through team emails or LinkedIn are generally not considered — [AI Research Track](https://agenticsystemslab.notion.site/AI-Research-Track-248b0f8bfec080c4833bda116f043078)
- Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship: you must find your own supervisor at the host institution, and applications without one are not considered — [SERI scholarships](https://www.sbfi.admin.ch/scholarships_eng)
- ELLIS exchange funding normally comes from the advisor(s) — [Cambridge ELLIS](https://www.ellis.eng.cam.ac.uk/?p=5527)

### Inferences
- Arriving with your own funding (DAAD, SGES, Ernst Mach, Marshall Plan) greatly increases acceptance, because visiting PhD students generally receive no salary (stated explicitly by ISTA).
- Lead time: the grants below need a host letter months ahead (DAAD and SGES calls close about 9–12 months before the start).

### Gaps
- No source on typical expectations such as joint publication, a talk or office space. Advice above is inference.

## Q4. Regional funding

### Takeaway
Germany: DAAD Research Stays (about EUR 1,400/month in 2026-era calls, 2–12 months). Switzerland: Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship Research Fellowship (CHF 2,450/month, 12 months from Sept, country-specific deadlines mostly in autumn). Austria: Ernst Mach Grant Worldwide (EUR 1,300/month, 1–9 months, deadline about 1 Feb, age limit 35) and Marshall Plan (Austria and US only).

### Cited Findings
- **DAAD Research Stays (Germany):** EUR 1,400/month plus insurance, travel and research allowances, for 2–12 months for doctoral candidates (2–6 for postdocs). The current call covers stays starting in 2027 — [DAAD database (via search snippet)](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database?detail=57742121); [DAAD 2026 call summary (ABG)](https://www.abg.asso.fr/en/article/2762/en-fellowships-for-phd-candidates-by-the-german-academic-exchange-service-daad-2762). An older cycle paid EUR 1,300/month for 1–6 months — [GradGermany](https://www.gradgermany.com/scholarships-germany/daad-research-grants-short-term). Deadlines vary by home country; one aggregator lists 24 Sep 2026 — [fundsforngos](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/individuals/submit-applications-for-research-grant-program/). Conflict: the figures come from aggregators, so check the official call for your country.
- **Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships, Research Fellowship 2027–28:** CHF 2,450/month (CHF 29,400 per year). Starts 1 Sep 2027 and lasts 12 months, not extendable. Requires a Master's by 31 Jul 2027 (30 Jun 2027 for ETH) and a host supervisor. Deadlines depend on country: Pakistan 31 Oct 2026, South Korea 10 Nov 2026, Bosnia 20 Nov 2026 (all passed); one list shows Guatemala 27 Nov 2026 — [SERI 2027–28](https://www.sbfi.admin.ch/scholarships_eng); [SERI at a glance](https://www.sbfi.admin.ch/en/swiss-government-excellence-scholarships-at-a-glance); [Swissnex Japan](https://swissnex.org/about-us/locations/tokyo/academia/eskas/); [Bosnia announcement (PDF)](https://pf.sum.ba/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Announcment-Swiss-Government-Excellence-Scholarship-2027-2028.pdf). No source supports the CHF 1,920 figure that is sometimes quoted.
- **Ernst Mach Grant Worldwide (Austria, OeAD/BMWF):** EUR 1,300/month for 1–9 months for a research stay at an Austrian university. Deadline 1 Feb 2026 for 2026/27, and likely 1 Feb 2027 for the next cycle. Age limit 35. Not meant to fund a regular degree — [OeAD Ernst Mach](https://www.oead.at/en/to-austria/grants-and-scholarships/ernst-mach-grant/); [OeAD FAQ](https://oead.at/en/study-research-teaching/overview-grants-and-scholarships/ernst-mach-grant/faq-ernst-mach-grant-worldwide); [PhDportal](https://www.phdportal.com/scholarships/497/ernst-mach-grant-worldwide.html); [OeAD 2026–27 overview](https://oead.at/en/news/article/2025/11/oead-scholarship-programmes-and-further-grants-to-austria-for-2026-2027)
- **Austrian Marshall Plan Foundation:** EUR 4,000–10,000 for Austrian and US students on research stays of at least 3 months, mainly in technical, natural-science and economics fields. Virtual stays get 40% (EUR 1,600–4,000). A 25th-anniversary call adds 25 extra fellowships for US students coming to Austria, plus an "Independence 250" call — [FH Salzburg](https://www.fh-salzburg.ac.at/en/international/international/scholarships/marshall-plan-scholarship); [JKU handout](https://jku.at/fileadmin/gruppen/50/Dokumente/MPS_Info_Handout); [Marshall Plan overview](https://www.marshallplan.at/overview-scholarships); [Independence 250](https://www.marshallplan.at/independence250); [BOKU anniversary call](https://boku.ac.at/news/newsitem/85012)
- OeAD Marietta Blau grant (outgoing, for students at Austrian universities) — [Uni Vienna call](https://fgga.univie.ac.at/en/service/third-party-funding-service-calls/details/news/call-oead-marietta-blau-grant-1/)

### Inferences
- The DAAD stipend (about EUR 1,400) roughly covers a Munich room (about EUR 800–1,000) plus basic living costs only tightly. It is more comfortable in Tübingen, Saarbrücken or Darmstadt. The SGES amount is designed around Swiss costs.

### Gaps
- Bavarian programs (e.g. BayHOST, BayFrance), Baden-Württemberg-STIPENDIUM, Erasmus+ traineeships, the Liechtenstein university (Uni Liechtenstein has no major AI hub) and Swiss National Science Foundation mobility (for Swiss-based PhDs going abroad) were not verified.

## Q5. Practicalities: cost of living, visas, language

### Takeaway
Munich furnished room: about EUR 800–1,000/month. German average shared-flat room: EUR 510 (WS 2026/27). Berlin, Hamburg median: about EUR 650. Zurich, Vienna, Tübingen and other figures were not found with 2026 sources.

- German average shared-flat (WG) room: EUR 510 in winter semester 2026/27, up 1% year on year (WG-Gesucht / Moses Mendelssohn Institute) — [WG-Gesucht WS 2026/27](https://www.wg-gesucht.de/en/presse/student-housing-costs/winter-semester-2026-2027-wg-room-rents); summer 2026: EUR 512 — [WG-Gesucht SS 2026](https://www.wg-gesucht.de/en/presse/student-housing-costs/summer-semester-2026)
- Munich: median room EUR 810 (Q1 2026) and average furnished room EUR 808 (Q4 2025) per HousingAnywhere. Erasmus Play gives about EUR 974 for a private room and EUR 1,139 for co-living — [HousingAnywhere index](https://housinganywhere.com/rent-index-by-city); [Q4 2025](https://housinganywhere.com/rent-index-by-city-Q4-2025); [Erasmus Play Munich](https://erasmusplay.com/en/market-trends/de/munchen)
- Munich sample listings, Oct–Dec 2026: EUR 660–800 warm rent; on Wunderflats EUR 700–780 plus EUR 110, minimum 6 months — [WG-Gesucht Munich](https://www.wg-gesucht.de/en/wg-zimmer-in-Munchen.90.0.1.0.html); [Wunderflats](https://wunderflats.com/g/wud8TcpZS7BqfsnN_MARq)

- Short-term furnished platforms (Wunderflats, HousingAnywhere, WG-Gesucht "Zwischenmiete") match 3–12-month stays. Expect a premium over long-term rents.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 rents for Zurich, Lausanne, Lugano, Basel, Vienna, Linz, Graz, Tübingen, Saarbrücken, Darmstadt, Freiburg, Karlsruhe, Heidelberg, Berlin (average), Dresden or Bonn. [background, unverified]: Zurich furnished rooms commonly cost CHF 1,000–1,600 and Vienna rooms EUR 500–750.
- Visa rules were not verified. [background, unverified]: EU/EFTA citizens need no visa anywhere in DACH, only address registration (and in CH, a permit for stays over 3 months, through the cantonal migration office). Non-EU visitors generally need a national D visa or a residence permit for stays over 90 days. Germany has a researcher permit under §18d AufenthG and a guest-scientist route. Switzerland requires the host institution to support an L/B permit. Austria uses a Researcher/Forscher Aufenthaltsbewilligung or a visa D, and OeAD guest-researcher services help. Language: English is the working language in all listed labs; German helps for housing and admin (French in Lausanne, Italian in Lugano).
