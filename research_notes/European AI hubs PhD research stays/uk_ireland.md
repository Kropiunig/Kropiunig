# UK & Ireland AI Hubs for Visiting PhD Research Stays (3–12 months) — as of Nov 2026

Method note: Web searches were run on 2026-11-24. Direct page fetches of ox.ac.uk and registryservices.ed.ac.uk were blocked in this session (network allowlist), so some figures come from search-result snippets of official pages rather than full-page reads. Items marked **[PK]** come from the researcher's prior knowledge (training data up to mid-2026) and were NOT re-verified this session; the URLs given for them are the groups' known homepages and should be checked before use. ## Q1. Hubs: city, strengths, flagship groups/PIs

### Takeaway
The "golden triangle" (Oxford, Cambridge, London: UCL/Imperial/KCL, plus GDM, Meta, Anthropic, OpenAI, Apollo and AISI in London) dominates, and Edinburgh is the strongest centre outside it (NLP, ML, multi-agent RL, blockchain via Kiayias, national supercomputing). For agentic/multi-agent systems, LLM evals and safety, the strongest fits are Oxford (FLAIR/Foerster, Wooldridge, OATML/Gal), UCL (DARK/Rocktäschel, Jun Wang), Cambridge (Prorok, CFI/Hernández-Orallo evals), Edinburgh, Southampton (agents, RAi UK), and London's safety ecosystem (AISI, Apollo, LISA, Cooperative AI Foundation). Ireland is smaller and more applied (Insight, ADAPT, CeADAR), but it is in the EU and still in Erasmus+.

## Q2. Formal visiting PhD programmes: fees, duration, deadlines, URLs

### Takeaway
UK universities charge visiting PhD students, sometimes heavily. Oxford's "Recognised Student" status costs roughly £12.6k–£15k per year (one third per term). Cambridge charges the full PhD composition fee for formal registration, or a lower departmental fee for informal registration. Edinburgh Informatics is free for visits up to 12 weeks, then charges a University flat rate. Most AI PIs host visitors informally, and fee waivers depend on the department and host.

### Cited Findings
- **Oxford, Recognised Student (non-award, up to 1 year):** 2025/26 annual rates are by subject, e.g. Anthropology £13,370, Archaeology £12,620, Statistics £14,350, Theology £15,040. Students staying less than a year pay one third per term. A later table (probably 2026/27) shows Statistics £14,920 and Theology £15,940. The CS/Engineering rates were not captured — [Oxford non-award fees](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/fees-and-other-charges/visiting-recognised-and-matriculated-non-award-students); [alt node](https://ox.ac.uk/node/17192)
- **Cambridge:** Visiting students come for a fixed period (typically up to 1 year) and stay registered at their home institution. Formally registered visitors pay a fee equal to the University Composition Fee for PhDs in that department. Informally registered visitors may pay a department-set fee. Applicants contact the department directly — [Cambridge postgraduate visiting students](https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses/visiting-students)
  - Departmental examples: Judge Business School £660/month (Oct 2026–Sep 2027, including summer; full month charged for partial months) — [JBS](https://www.jbs.cam.ac.uk/phd-research-masters/visiting-research-students/); Sociology £1,200/term — [Sociology](https://www.sociology.cam.ac.uk/visiting-phd-students-department-sociology); MMLL £2,400/year, £720/term or £90/week — [MMLL](https://www.mmll.cam.ac.uk/prospective-students/visiting-students); Development Studies bench fee £1,000/month — [DevStudies](https://www.devstudies.cam.ac.uk/academicvisitors/what-we-provide). Only one visit is allowed per rolling 12 months, with no split visits — [Informatics PGR visiting policy](https://web.inf.ed.ac.uk/node/741). ### Inferences
- For a 3-month visit, Edinburgh's 12-week fee-free window is a strong cost advantage. At Oxford, a 1-term Recognised Student stay costs about £4.2k–5.3k (one third of the annual rate).

- An industry internship (GDM, MSR, Meta) or a paid safety fellowship (LASR, MATS, ERA) is often cheaper than a formal academic visit, because it pays a stipend.

## Q3. Visa routes (UK and Ireland)

### Takeaway
UK: up to 6 months, a PhD student enrolled abroad can use the Standard Visitor route, which allows a short research piece relevant to the overseas course if a UK HEI has accepted them. EU and other non-visa nationals need only an ETA. Over 6 months requires a Student visa (CAS from a licensed sponsor, £776/yr IHS) or a sponsored Government Authorised Exchange (GAE) research route (higher IHS). The 12-month "Academic Visitor" sub-route is for academics employed overseas, not PhD students. Ireland: EU/EEA/Swiss and UK citizens need nothing. Non-EU stays over 90 days need a long-stay D visa or a Hosting Agreement (scientific researcher) plus IRP registration.

### Cited Findings
- Standard Visitor: up to 6 months for "a short piece of research that's relevant to your course overseas", if accepted by a UK HEI. Over 6 months requires a different visa (e.g. Student) — [GOV.UK visit to study](https://www.gov.uk/standard-visitor/visit-to-study)
- Visitors cannot extend beyond 6 months, cannot switch visas in-country, and cannot work, paid or unpaid. Frequent, successive visitor stays are discouraged — [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/students/immigration-and-visas/standard-visitor-visa-study-eta-scheme); [Edinburgh conditions](https://registryservices.ed.ac.uk/immigration/applying-for-visa/other-visa-types/standard-visitor/conditions); [Imperial](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/students/international-students/visas-and-immigration/coming-to-the-uk-as-a-visitor/)
- For stays under 6 months, students can choose Standard Visitor or Student — [Reading](https://www.reading.ac.uk/essentials/International/Visa-and-immigration/Standard-Visitor-visa)
- Academic Visitor (up to 12 months) requires employment by an overseas HEI in a teaching or research role. One university notes research students are not eligible. ATAS may be required for research — [Cambridge HR](https://www.hr.admin.cam.ac.uk/hr-services/visas-immigration/visiting-uk-0/standard-visitor-academic-category); [Southampton guidance](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/~assets/doc/hr/Guidance%20-%20Visa%20Routes%20for%20International%20Visitors.pdf)
- Standard Visitor fees vary by source (£127 for 6 months, or £200–220 for 12 months), so check gov.uk — [DavidsonMorris](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/academic-visitor-visa/) (third-party)
- IHS: £776/yr for students (since April 2024) and £1,035/yr for other routes. Visitors pay no IHS — [GOV.UK IHS](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay); [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/study/international/immigration-and-visas/student-visas/healthcare-fee)
- Temporary Work – GAE main applicant fee: £298, then £319 from 9 April 2025. The Home Office raised fees again in April 2026, with new amounts not captured — [Home Office fees Apr 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/visa-regulations-revised-table/home-office-immigration-and-nationality-fees-9-april-2025); [Migration Observatory](https://migrationobservatory.ox.ac.uk/resources/commentaries/qa-immigration-fees-in-the-uk/)
- **[PK]**: The GAE route via the UKRI "Science, Research and Academia" scheme allows sponsored researchers (including visiting PhD researchers if the host university uses it) for up to 24 months. It requires a host Certificate of Sponsorship and is the usual route for funded or paid visiting researchers over 6 months. The ETA for visa-exempt nationals (EU from April 2025) cost about £16 in 2025. The Student visa outside the UK cost about £524 from April 2025. Verify the April 2026 rises.
- Ireland: the Hosting Agreement (scientific researcher) is for non-EU researchers at accredited institutions, issued by university HR. Visa-required nationals also need an Employment (Scientific Researcher) entry visa, applied for up to 3 months before travel. Some visiting academics use Stamp 0. Family may accompany the researcher — [Irish Immigration Service](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-work-in-ireland/what-are-my-work-visa-options/applying-for-a-long-stay-employment-visa/scientific-researcher/); [TCD hosting agreements](https://www.tcd.ie/hr/resourcing/applicant/hosting-agreements.php); [EU Immigration Portal](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/researcher-ireland_en); [Galway FAQ](https://universityofgalway.ie/human-resources/recruitment-and-selection/applicant-information/hostingagreementfaqs/)
- **[PK]**: In Ireland, non-EU stays under 90 days use a short-stay C visa where required. Stays over 90 days need IRP registration (€300). EU/EEA/Swiss citizens have free movement, and UK/Irish citizens have Common Travel Area rights.

### Inferences
- An EU PhD student's simplest 3–6-month UK stay is: an ETA plus a Standard Visitor stay with a host acceptance letter, no salary from the UK host, and possibly a fee.
- A 6–12-month UK stay needs a Student visa (a formal visiting-student registration with CAS, so likely a fee) or GAE sponsorship by the host.
- Ireland is visa-free for EU citizens.

### Gaps
- April 2026 Home Office fee levels (visitor, student, GAE, ETA) were not verified.
- Whether a specific CS PhD topic triggers ATAS for a given nationality was not checked.

## Q4. Funding for incoming visitors; Erasmus+

### Takeaway
Big change: the UK rejoins Erasmus+ for 2027, from the 2027/28 academic year. An EU PhD student could then potentially fund a UK stay through an Erasmus+ traineeship or doctoral mobility from their home university. Participation beyond 2027 (the 2028–34 cycle) is not yet agreed. Ireland remains a full Erasmus+ member. Otherwise, funding is mostly from the home institution, host PIs, paid industry internships, paid safety fellowships, or the AISI Alignment Project (for projects, not visits).

### Cited Findings
- The UK and EU finalised the agreement for the UK to join Erasmus+ in 2027. The UK contributes about £570m for 2027/28, a 30% discount. Participation in 2028–2034 still has to be agreed. The British Council will be the national agency, and over 100,000 UK participants are expected in year 1 — [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-and-eu-finalise-agreement-to-bring-uk-into-erasmus-in-2027); [DAAD](https://www.daad.de/en/press-releases/2026/vereinigtes-koenigreich-kehrt-2027-zu-erasmus-zurueck/); [FE Week](https://feweek.co.uk/uk-to-rejoin-erasmus-in-2027/); [ICEF](https://monitor.icef.com/?p=47499); [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2025/12/17/uk-to-rejoin-eus-erasmus-student-mobility-fund-in-2027)
- The UK's own Turing Scheme funds outbound UK student mobility, not incoming visitors — [Wikipedia: Turing scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_scheme)
- The AISI Alignment Project (£27m pool; £50k–£1m grants, worldwide eligibility) could fund a collaborative project with a UK host — see Q1.
- Paid placements: Turing Internship Network (UK-enrolled only), GDM Student Researcher, MSR Cambridge internships, Meta FAIR internships, LASR (£15k/13 weeks), ERA (about £10k/10 weeks), MATS ($5k/month) — see Q1/Q2.
- **[PK]**: Royal Society International Exchanges grants (up to about £12k) are applied for by a UK-based PI with an overseas collaborator and can fund visits. MSCA Doctoral Network secondments can fund stays at UK partners (the UK is associated to Horizon Europe since 2024). The ELLIS PhD exchange is funded by the home or host unit.

### Inferences
- A stay starting from autumn 2027 should check whether the home university's Erasmus+ inter-institutional or traineeship agreements now cover UK hosts.
- Ireland is the safest Erasmus+-funded option in 2026–27.

### Gaps
- How UK HEIs will run incoming Erasmus+ doctoral traineeships in 2027/28 is not yet detailed.
- Ireland-specific incoming traineeship rules were not found.

## Q5. Cost of living (rent)

### Takeaway
London is the most expensive (about £1,620–£2,100 for a 1-bed, about £1,000 for a room in inner London). Oxford, Cambridge and Edinburgh are about £1,250–1,350 for a 1-bed and £790–£900 for a room. Dublin is the most expensive of all: about €2,000+ for a 1-bed and €2,600+ for a 2-bed.

### Cited Findings
- SpareRoom Q3 2026 room rents: Oxford £834, Cambridge £792, Edinburgh £894; inner London £1,002, outer London £800 — [SpareRoom Q3 2026](https://www.spareroom.co.uk/statistics/uk-quarterly-rental-index-q3-2026)
- SpareRoom Q2 2026: London £979, Edinburgh £834, Oxford £814 — [SpareRoom Q2 2026](https://www.spareroom.co.uk/statistics/uk-quarterly-rental-index-q2-2026)
- 1-bed (2026, internally inconsistent secondary source): London about £1,750 (range £1,650–2,100), Oxford £1,350, Cambridge £1,280, Edinburgh £1,000–1,300 — [Pocketwise](https://pocketwise.co.uk/personal-finance/renting/)
- London 1-bed average private rent was £1,620 in 2024/25 — [Trust for London](https://www.trustforlondon.org.uk/data/average-london-rents/)
- Edinburgh has been ranked the UK's least affordable city for tenants — [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/news/cost-living/edinburgh-ranked-uks-least-affordable-city-for-tenants-with-renters-more-than-1k/)
- Dublin (Daft.ie): Q2 2026 2-bed apartment €2,634 (national €2,204). Q1 2026 1-bed apartment about €2,012. Dublin city apartments almost €2,700 (Feb 2026). Rents rose a record 4.4% in Q1 2026 after new rules came in, and rental supply is at a 20-year low — [Daft Q2 2026](https://www.daft.ie/report/ronan-lyons-2018q1-rental); [Daft Q1 2026 PDF](https://www.rte.ie/documents/news/2026/05/daft.ie-rental-report-2026q1.pdf); [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/05/20/rents-surge-by-record-44-in-quarter-as-new-tenancy-rules-kick-in/); [Irish Times supply](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/02/24/supply-of-homes-to-rent-in-february-hits-by-far-its-lowest-level-in-20-years/)

### Inferences
- Short-let premiums for 3–6-month stays make these figures a floor. College or university short-term accommodation (Oxford and Cambridge colleges, Edinburgh visitor housing) is worth requesting through the host.

### Gaps
- No Galway, Manchester, Bristol or other regional figures were gathered (they are generally cheaper, at about £600–750 per room) **[PK]**.
- No short-let-specific data was found.
