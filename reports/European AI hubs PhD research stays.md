# Plan a European AI research stay that works

The best 3–12 month visiting-PhD stay in Europe almost never comes from a call for applications. Nearly everywhere, it comes from a host PI who agrees to have you, plus funding you bring yourself. The strongest hubs are Tübingen, Zurich/Lausanne, London/Oxford/Cambridge, Paris, Amsterdam/Delft, Munich, Saarbrücken, Edinburgh, Copenhagen and Helsinki. For an agentic/multi-agent, evals and AI x blockchain profile with heavy open-source credentials, the best verified matches are:

- **Sahar Abdelnabi (COMPASS) and Maksym Andriushchenko (AISA) at the ELLIS Institute Tübingen**: multi-agent safety, agent alignment, evals.
- **Florian Tramèr's SPY Lab at ETH Zurich**: agent security; AgentDojo and CaMeL.
- **Arthur Gervais at UCL**: LLM agents for DeFi exploit generation.
- **Jakob Foerster's FLAIR at Oxford**: multi-agent LLM coordination.
- **UK AISI's open-source Inspect ecosystem** as a credibility-building on-ramp.

Very few hubs run a formal visiting-PhD scheme. The exceptions are ISTA's year-round internships, Lamarr's visiting-researcher programme, Bosch's 3–12 month PhD sabbatical, IMDEA Software's "visiting PhD" posts, EPFL's de facto visitor hosting, and the ELLIS ≥6-month exchange, which is reserved for ELLIS students. So the decisive variable is the funding stack.

If you are enrolled at an Austrian university (likely, but not established here), four schemes do most of the work:

- **OeAD Marietta Blau grant**: 6–12 months, about EUR 1,600–1,800 per month (figures conflict). The next deadline is probably 1 Feb 2027.
- **Erasmus+ traineeship**: about EUR 540–750 per month. TU Wien caps funding at 4 months.
- **Uni Wien KWA or TU Wien KUWI**: for 1–3 month stays.
- **COST STSMs**: up to 180 days for early-career researchers.

Paid alternatives often beat academic visits on money: industry PhD internships (Google DeepMind, Microsoft Research, Bosch, Aleph Alpha, SAP) and AI-safety fellowships (MATS, LASR, ERA). The two dates that matter most in the next 12 months are the probable 1 Feb 2027 Marietta Blau deadline and the UK's re-entry to Erasmus+ for 2027/28, which reopens funded UK stays. Many details below come from search snippets rather than full primary pages, or from researchers' prior knowledge. Those items are flagged **[U]** (unverified) or **[PK]** (prior knowledge), and conflicting figures are shown side by side rather than resolved by fiat.

## Europe's AI map has a dozen strong hubs but few open doors

Europe's AI research is organised around national centres layered on top of the ELLIS network. ELLIS is the main pan-European structure. Its PhD & Postdoc Program requires Academic Track students to spend **at least 6 months with a co-advisor in a different European country**, and the time can be split ([ELLIS PhD & Postdoc](https://ellis.eu/research/phd-postdoc)). The 2026 call (for 2027 entry) closed on **31 Oct 2026**, and reference letters are due **25 Nov 2026**. Interviews run Jan–Feb 2027 and decisions come in late Feb/Mar 2027 ([ELLIS call 2026](https://ellis.eu/news/ellis-phd-program-call-for-applications-2026)).

The network keeps growing. A dated snapshot of ellis.eu showed "42 Units, 1 Associate Unit, 2 Institutes, 17 Countries" ([ELLIS sites](https://ellis.eu/sites)). In 2026, seven new units were added: Czechia, Denmark, Franconia, Grenoble, NRW, Slovenia and Sweden ([ELLIS news](https://ellis.eu/news/seven-new-units-join-the-ellis-network-czechia-denmark-franconia-grenoble-nrw-slovenia-sweden)). Treat ellis.eu/sites as the authoritative list. The full 2026 roster was not verified in this research, because the site could not be fetched.

The research agrees on three structural facts:

- **Visiting PhD students are generally unpaid guests.** ISTA states it explicitly: visitors "are not employees and get no salary" ([ISTA Scientific Internships](https://phd.ista.ac.at/scientific-internships/)).
- **ELLIS exchange money normally comes from the advisors.** ELLIS itself funds only small travel grants ([Cambridge ELLIS](https://www.ellis.eng.cam.ac.uk/?p=5527)).
- **Big EU lighthouse networks are winding down.** ELISE's mobility programme (up to EUR 2,500 travel) ran 2020–2023 and has ended ([ELISE T&C](https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/5f55e90f6a7294f66f94d30d/61ea82242923767d20c3e03c_Terms%20and%20Conditions%20of%20the%20ELISE%20Mobility%20Program%20for%20PhDs%20and%20Postdocs.pdf)). ELSA was extended only to **end of Aug 2026** ([CISPA](https://cispa.de/en/elsa-extension)).
- **Newer vehicles are where fresh mobility money is likelier to appear.** These are ELLIOT (EUR 25M, Jul 2025–Jun 2029) ([idw](https://idw-online.de/en/news853914)), ELIAS, and the Horizon Europe RAISE "AI for science" networks ([CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/programme/id/HORIZON_HORIZON-RAISE-2026-01-01)). This is an inference.

There is also an ELIAS mobility grant figure of up to EUR 2,400 for 3–6 month visits, but it appears only on an aggregator and is **[U]** ([scholarshipsads](https://www.scholarshipsads.com/elias-mobility-program-travel-grants)).

The catalogue below covers every European region. PI names marked [PK] or [U] are leads to check, not facts: AI faculty move often. For example, Stefano Albrecht has left Edinburgh for NTU Singapore ([NTU news](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/computing/news-events/news/detail/ntu-ccds-professor-publishes-ai-book-with-cambridge-university-press)).

### DACH: Tübingen, Zurich and Lausanne lead for agents and safety

Germany, Switzerland and Austria have the densest cluster for agentic and trustworthy AI.

**ELLIS Institute Tübingen.** Its groups cover AI safety, cooperative machine intelligence, AutoML, robust ML and more. PIs include Bernhard Schölkopf, Frank Hutter, Jonas Geiping, Wieland Brendel, Antonio Orvieto, Celestine Mendler-Dünner and Rediet Abebe ([ELLIS Tübingen research groups](https://institute-tue.ellis.eu/research-groups)). Two new PIs, from Sep–Oct 2025, are the central matches for this profile:

- **Andriushchenko's AISA group**: alignment of autonomous LLM agents and rigorous evaluations.
- **Abdelnabi's COMPASS group**: safe, steerable AI agents and cooperative multi-agent systems.

Sources: [New PIs news](https://institut-tue.ellis.eu/news/new-principal-investigators-join-the-ellis-institute-tubingen); [ELLIS AISA](https://institute-tue.ellis.eu/aisa).

**Saarbrücken.** It combines CISPA, MPI-SWS, MPI-INF and DFKI. The Max Planck institutes run CS research internships of roughly 12–14 weeks, aimed mostly at students before a PhD ([CIS internships](https://cis.mpg.de/internships)). CISPA's internships explicitly exclude PhD students ([CISPA YRIP](https://career.cispa.de/yrip.html)). DFKI's Agents and Simulated Reality department (Slusallek) covers multi-agent simulation, and DFKI routes guest inquiries to department contacts ([DFKI](https://www.dfki.de/index.php?id=44&L=1)).

**Germany's most structured visitor schemes** are these:

- **Lamarr Institute Visiting Researcher Program** (Bonn/Dortmund). PIs invite PhD students and postdocs for funded stays ([Lamarr](https://lamarr-institute.org/visiting-researcher-program/)).
- **TU Darmstadt/hessian.AI Future Talent Guest Stay.** Lasts 1–6 weeks, runs until **31 Dec 2026**, and stays of 3 months or more need a TU Hosting Agreement ([TU Darmstadt](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/forschen/wissenschaftlicher_nachwuchs_tu/post_docs/future_talents/index.en.jsp)).
- **Helmholtz Visiting Researcher Grant.** Its last confirmed round was 2024 ([Research in Germany](https://research-in-germany.org/en/research-funding/funding-programmes/helmholtz-visiting-researcher-grant.html)).

**Programmes that do not take incoming visitors.** MCML Munich's AI X-Change is outbound-only ([MCML](https://mcml.ai/opportunities/for-members/research-x-change/)). The Tübingen AI Center has no visiting-PhD scheme ([Uni Tübingen](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/128980)).

**Switzerland.** ETH's AI Center fellowships are full PhD positions ([jobup](https://www.jobup.ch/en/jobs/detail/062b0960-3c7a-47a1-a1b7-03e8626837de)). Visits run through individual chairs:

- **Tramèr's SPY Lab** is the leading verified agent-security group ([ethz-spylab GitHub](https://github.com/ethz-spylab)).
- **The ETH Agentic Systems Lab** has an "Affiliate Visiting Researcher Track" for PhD researchers. You apply via a form and interview, and cold emails to team members are generally not considered ([ASL track](https://agenticsystemslab.notion.site/Affiliate-Visiting-Researcher-Track-2c9b0f8bfec0807f9021caf74758cc5a)).
- **The EPFL AI Center** has the clearest public evidence of hosting visiting PhDs on agents and LLMs. Its fellows page lists visitors on multi-agent orchestration (hosts Kamgarpour/Cevher) and LLM training (Jaggi) ([EPFL AI Center fellows](https://ai.epfl.ch/about/fellows-researchers/)).
- **Idiap (Martigny)** offers 6–18 month internships through its portal ([Idiap](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/idiap/student-projects/)).
- **IDSIA (Lugano)** has no visiting programme ([SUPSI IDSIA](https://www.supsi.ch/en/idsia)).
- **The Swiss AI Initiative** released Apertus 1.5 (8B/70B) on 24 Jul 2026, and CSCS runs an inference API with an agentic endpoint for Swiss academics ([CSCS](https://www.cscs.ch/science/computer-science-hpc/2026/apertus-15-building-the-next-generation-of-open-ai-infrastructure)).

**Austria.** It matters both as a possible home base and as a host:

- **ISTA (Klosterneuburg).** Groups host visiting PhDs for **2–12 months, year-round, case by case**. Students holding Erasmus+ or similar can join for 2–6 months ([ISTA](https://phd.ista.ac.at/scientific-internships/)). Relevant groups: Alistarh (efficient LLMs) and Lampert, whose ASIDE work at ICLR 2026 separates instructions from data, which is relevant to prompt injection ([ISTA ELLIS publications](https://ellis.pages.ista.ac.at/publications/)).
- **University of Vienna.** Weippl's group works on security of LLM-based and agentic systems ([sec.cs.univie.ac.at](https://sec.cs.univie.ac.at/research/focus)). Martin Pawelczyk works on "robust alignment and safety in multi-agent LLM systems" ([Uni Wien](https://www.univie.ac.at/en/news/new-professorships/details/pawelczyk-martin)).
- **TU Graz ISEC.** Phillip Rieger opened a Secure AI Systems lab on 1 Sep 2026 ([ISEC](https://www.isec.tugraz.at/?p=23609)).
- **TU Wien CAIML.** Covers symbolic AI, ML and explainable AI ([CAIML](https://caiml.dbai.tuwien.ac.at)).
- **JKU Linz.** Its LIT AI Lab cooperates with Hochreiter's NXAI on xLSTM ([JKU](https://www.jku.at/news-events/detail/news/ai-made-in-europe-spitzenforscher-sepp-hochreiter-und-sein-xlstm-erhalten-unternehmerische-verstaerkung-fuer-europaeisches-large-language-model/)).

### UK and Ireland: the golden triangle plus London's safety ecosystem

Oxford, Cambridge and London dominate, and Edinburgh is the strongest centre outside them. The Oxford ELLIS unit expanded in 2026 into a regional ecosystem that adds Warwick and the Ellison Institute ([ELLIS Oxford](https://www.ellis.ox.ac.uk/)).

**Verified multi-agent and agentic work:**

- **Foerster (FLAIR, Oxford).** His zero-shot coordination study finds frontier LLM agents fail to coordinate with novel partners. The Nov 2026 date comes from a secondary source and is [U] ([cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/oxford-study-llm-agents-coordination-failures/)). He also co-authored the Cooperative AI Foundation's "Multi-Agent Risks from Advanced AI" report ([arXiv 2502.14143](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2502.14143)).
- **Rocktäschel (UCL DARK).** Works on open-endedness and self-improving agents ([ICLR 2025 talk](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/invited-talk/10000536)).

**Further leads [PK]:** Wooldridge, Gal (OATML), Whiteson and Torr (Oxford); Prorok, Hernández-Orallo (AI evaluation, CFI), Vlachos and Weller (Cambridge); Toni, Shanahan, Cully and Knottenbelt (Imperial); Yali Du and Elizabeth Black (KCL); Kiayias (Edinburgh, blockchain); Southampton's agents group.

**The Alan Turing Institute is now a riskier generic-ML host.** In Oct 2025 it closed, spun out or completed 78 projects and refocused on defence and national security ([Turing](https://www.turing.ac.uk/news/turing-announces-new-science-and-innovation-programme-strengthen-uk-success-and-resilience); [Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366633598/Alan-Turing-Institute-refocuses-on-security-following-Peter-Kyle-intervention)).

**UK universities charge visitors.**

- **Oxford "Recognised Student" status** costs roughly **GBP 12.6k–15.9k per year by subject**, charged one third per term. The CS and Engineering rates were not captured ([Oxford fees](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/fees-and-other-charges/visiting-recognised-and-matriculated-non-award-students)).
- **Cambridge** charges the PhD composition fee for formal registration, or a department-set fee for informal registration ([Cambridge](https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses/visiting-students)). Examples range from GBP 90/week to GBP 1,000/month ([Judge](https://www.jbs.cam.ac.uk/phd-research-masters/visiting-research-students/); [DevStudies](https://www.devstudies.cam.ac.uk/academicvisitors/what-we-provide)).
- **Edinburgh Informatics** is **free for visits up to 12 weeks**, then charges a flat University fee ([Edinburgh policy](https://web.inf.ed.ac.uk/node/741)).

**Ireland** remains in the EU and Erasmus+, but it is more applied: Insight, ADAPT and CeADAR ([Insight](https://www.insight-centre.org/?p=9944)). Dublin rents are the highest in the dataset (see the cost table).

## Formal visiting routes are rare, so the host PI and your own money decide

Across all six regions surveyed, only a short list publishes a structured route that a PhD student enrolled elsewhere can actually use. **ISTA's Scientific Internships** (2–12 months, year-round, unpaid) and the **EPFL AI Center** (de facto, no call) are the clearest academic doors. **Lamarr's Visiting Researcher Program** is funded, but you need an invitation from a Lamarr PI. **IMDEA Software's periodic visiting-PhD posts** pay competitive stipends. On the industry side, **Bosch's PhD Industry Sabbatical** (3–12 months) ([aijobs](https://aijobs.net/job/173219-phd-industry-sabbatical-at-the-bosch-center-for-artificial-intelligence/)) is the closest analogue to an academic visit. The **ETH Agentic Systems Lab visitor track** and **Idiap internships** add two more Swiss options.

Everything else follows the same mechanism. A PI agrees to host you and issues an invitation letter or hosting agreement. You then bring funding from your home side, ELLIS exchange money if you are an ELLIS student, Erasmus+, COST or a national grant.

Paid alternatives deserve equal weight, because they invert the economics:

- **Google DeepMind.** "Research Scientist PhD Intern 2027" roles last 13–17 weeks in Switzerland, France or Germany, starting May–Jul 2027, and are "not eligible for immigration sponsorship" ([Google listing](https://jobs.anitab.org/companies/google-24698/jobs/90220301-research-scientist-phd-intern-2027)). The Student Researcher programme runs 12–24 weeks at a minimum of 4 days per week ([DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/careers/internships/)). One listing gave a 23 Oct 2026 deadline, which has passed. Other deadlines show only a truncated "Dec." and are [U].
- **AI-safety fellowships pay well for agent and eval work.** LASR Labs pays GBP 15,000 for 13 weeks in London ([OpportunityDesk](https://opportunitydesk.org/2026/08/27/london-ai-safety-research-labs-winter-2027/)). MATS offered USD 5,000/month plus USD 8,000/month compute in its Autumn 2026 track ([LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/GufBJNg62gkpbnE4Q/mats-autumn-2026-fellowship-applications-now-open-apply-by)). ERA:AI Cambridge runs 10-week fellowships, with a reported GBP 10,000 stipend for Winter 2027, an aggregator figure and [U] ([ERA](https://erafellowship.org/fellowship)).

## Funding stacks around one big grant plus top-ups

### ELLIS, Erasmus+, MSCA and COST form the European layer

**ELLIS** is useful only if you are already an ELLIS student, or your supervisor can tap an ELLIS unit's or project's mobility budget. In June 2026, **G-Research funded 24 ELLIS PhD students' 6-month exchanges** ([ELLIS](https://ellis.eu/news/g-research-invests-in-ellis-phd-program-to-support-the-next-generation-of-european-ai-talent)). The ELLIS mobility page takes applications at phd@ellis.eu ([ELLIS mobility](https://ellis.eu/mobility-programs)). A page mentioning a **EUR 1,300/month stipend plus relocation package** for stays in Germany is undated and [U] ([ELLIS mobility fund](https://ellis.eu/mobility-fund)). The 2026 ELIAS mobility grants for the Lisbon Doctoral Symposium were limited to ELLIS students at German ELIZA sites ([EDS 2026](https://sites.google.com/view/eds2026lisbon/registration/mobility-funding)).

**Erasmus+ KA131** is the most universally usable source for a student enrolled at an EU programme-country university. Doctoral candidates can do:

- **Short-term mobility of 5–30 days**, at about EUR 79/day for days 1–14 ([Uni Siegen](https://www.uni-siegen.de/en/erasmus-grants)).
- **Long-term study or traineeship mobility of 2–12 months**, with a 12-month cap per cycle. Traineeships can be at research institutes or companies ([gov.uk guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-erasmus-programme/erasmus-programme-guidance-for-higher-education-he)).

Monthly traineeship rates for 2026–27 conflict by sending institution. For Group 1 destinations (AT, DE, FR, IT and others), Utrecht and Leiden give **EUR 540**, VUB **EUR 569**, Siegen **EUR 600**, and BTU and Hamburg **EUR 750** ([VUB](https://www.vub.be/sites/default/files/2026-07/erasmus%2B%20grants%20TRAINEESHIPS%202026%202027.pdf); [Uni Hamburg](https://www.uni-hamburg.de/internationales/studierende/outgoing/auslandspraktikum/auslandspraktikum-erasmus.html)). Group 2 (ES, PT) is about EUR 480 ([Leiden](https://www.student.universiteitleiden.nl/en/scholarships/sea/erasmus-for-traineeships?cd=biology-msc)). Budgets cap funded months: Utrecht about 3 months and 22 days, and **TU Wien 4 months** ([TU Wien](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/international/studying-abroad/mobility-programmes/erasmus-traineeships)).

Two geographic caveats:

- **The UK rejoins Erasmus+ from 2027/28.** It contributes about GBP 570M for that year, and participation from 2028 onward is not yet agreed ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-and-eu-finalise-agreement-to-bring-uk-into-erasmus-in-2027)). This reopens Erasmus-funded stays at UCL, Oxford or Edinburgh from autumn 2027.
- **Switzerland is not a full programme country.** Swiss exchanges run through SEMP [PK].

**MSCA Staff Exchanges** pay **EUR 2,870 per person-month** in the 2026 call ([UKRO](https://www.ukro.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Presentation-MSCA-SE-2026-UK-24-02-26.pdf)). The 2026 deadline was 16 Apr 2026 ([MSCA](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/calls/msca-staff-exchanges-2026)), and a 2027 call page exists ([MSCA SE 2027](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/funding/msca-staff-exchanges-2027)). You benefit only if your group is already a partner in a funded SE or Doctoral Network project. Secondments must be full-time ([FFG](https://www.ffg.at/sites/default/files/2026-10/MSCA_SE_2026_27_BF_Update_Call_27_0.pdf)).

**COST STSMs** are the most accessible small grant. They last **5–90 days, or up to 180 days for early-career researchers** ([UCC STOCHASTICA](https://www.ucc.ie/en/stochastica/grants/short-termscientificmissions/)), with per-Action caps around EUR 2,000–4,000. The ONWARD call document states both EUR 2,000 and EUR 4,000 ([ONWARD](https://www.cost.eu/uploads/2025/12/Call-STSM-Grants-CA24140-ONWARD.docx)). Relevant Actions include CA25157 (AI Governance), CA25153 (Responsible Privacy for and with AI) and CA24136 (Control Theory and ML) ([COST](https://www.cost.eu/cost-action/page/5/)). Join a working group via e-COST first. Calls come within each Nov–Oct grant period.

## Application playbook: host first, money second, visa third

**Start 9–12 months before the stay.** Start 12+ months ahead where deadlines sit 5–7 months before the start date: Marietta Blau, Swiss ESKAS, Eiffel, and industry internships, which post in Aug–Oct for May–Jul starts. Almost every grant requires a host letter, so secure that first.

**The PI email.** This guidance is synthesised from the notes and is largely [PK]:

- **Subject line:** "Research visit request: [topic], [dates], self-funded via [grant]".
- **Opening:** one sentence on who you are, your supervisor and your institution.
- **Fit:** two or three sentences tying a specific recent paper of theirs to a concrete project.
- **Logistics:** proposed dates, and a funding line showing no cost to the host.
- **Ask:** a short call and an invitation letter.
- **Attachments:** CV and a one-page project sketch.
- **Supervisor:** ideally CC'd, or sending the email themselves.

**Typical documents:** a 2–5 page work plan with timeline, CV, host invitation letter, home supervisor approval, doctoral agreement, Erasmus+ learning agreement for traineeships, budget, and a final report afterwards.

### Lab-specific rules

- **ETH Agentic Systems Lab** ignores cold emails and requires its form ([ASL](https://agenticsystemslab.notion.site/AI-Research-Track-248b0f8bfec080c4833bda116f043078)).
- **Swiss ESKAS** rejects applications that do not name a host supervisor ([SERI](https://www.sbfi.admin.ch/scholarships_eng)).
- **Edinburgh** allows only one visit per rolling 12 months, with no split visits ([Edinburgh](https://web.inf.ed.ac.uk/node/741)).

### Visas

For **EU/EEA citizens**, no visa is needed anywhere in the EU, EEA or Switzerland. Register locally for stays over 3 months. Switzerland additionally requires a cantonal permit for stays over 3 months [PK].

For **non-EU citizens**, the EU Researchers Directive 2016/801 applies:

- A host signs a **hosting agreement**, which is required for stays over 3 months ([EURAXESS](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/information)).
- A researcher permit from one member state allows **up to 180 days in any 360 in a second state**. Ireland and Denmark are not bound by the Directive ([IND](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/short-term-mobility-for-researchers-directive-eu-2016801); [Art. 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2016/801/article/28/data.htm)).

Country-specific points:

- **France:** passeport talent – chercheur needs funding around **EUR 1,768/month**, and below that you fall back to a student visa ([Paris Cité](https://u-paris.fr/doctorat/titre-de-sejour/)).
- **Netherlands:** the IND income threshold is **EUR 1,767/month** for Jul 2026–Jan 2027 ([WUR](https://www.wur.nl/en/education/phd-programme/visa-and-residence-permit)).
- **Sweden:** since **11 Jun 2026**, visiting doctoral students can get a research residence permit for 3–48 months. Maintenance must be at least SEK 9,700/month ([KI](https://news.ki.se/new-migration-regulations-for-researchers-and-doctoral-students)).
- **Italy:** a research nulla osta is needed for stays over 90 days. Start at least 3 months ahead ([EU Immigration Portal](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/researcher-italy_en)).
- **Ireland:** a hosting agreement plus IRP registration ([Irish Immigration](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-work-in-ireland/what-are-my-work-visa-options/applying-for-a-long-stay-employment-visa/scientific-researcher/)).

For the **UK**:

- **Standard Visitor:** allows **up to 6 months** of research relevant to your overseas course, once a UK HEI accepts you. EU nationals need only an ETA. You cannot work or extend ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/standard-visitor/visit-to-study); [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/students/immigration-and-visas/standard-visitor-visa-study-eta-scheme)).
- **Beyond 6 months:** you need a Student visa (IHS GBP 776/year) or sponsored GAE ([GOV.UK IHS](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay)).
- **Academic Visitor** routes exclude research students ([Cambridge HR](https://www.hr.admin.cam.ac.uk/hr-services/visas-immigration/visiting-uk-0/standard-visitor-academic-category)).

**Funding level sets visa feasibility.** A non-EU visitor on Erasmus+ alone will fail the French and Dutch thresholds.

### Insurance, social security, tax and housing

All of this is [PK] and should be verified with your HR and international office:

- **Insurance:** carry an EHIC, plus private top-up for liability and repatriation. Erasmus+ traineeships require accident and liability cover.
- **Social security:** if you remain employed in Austria while posted abroad, request an **A1 portable document from ÖGK** to stay in the Austrian system.
- **Tax:** tax residency normally stays at home for stays under 183 days.
- **Housing:** request guest housing via the host (ETH/EPFL/MPG guest houses, Oxbridge colleges, Edinburgh visitor housing) before turning to platforms.

**What the stipends buy.** A Marietta Blau grant (EUR 1,600–1,800) comfortably covers Tübingen, where an average German room is about EUR 510. It covers Munich or Paris only tightly, and in Zurich (furnished rooms around CHF 1,460) or London it leaves almost nothing for food. Erasmus+ (EUR 540–750) never covers rent in a top-tier city on its own. These are inferences from the figures above.

That asymmetry is the strongest practical argument for Tübingen over Zurich or London when the funding is a scholarship rather than a salary. It is also an argument for stacking a COST STSM or KWA/KUWI on top for the first months.

## Conclusion

Most guides frame the problem as finding the right programme. The research suggests a different one: assembling a funding stack that a host can say yes to at zero cost. Then you pick the city where that stack still pays the rent. On that logic, a scholarship-funded stay favours Tübingen and the mid-cost German, Austrian, Czech and Iberian hubs. Zurich, London and Dublin make sense mainly with a salary (industry internship, MSCA secondment, paid safety fellowship) or a Swiss-scale grant. Several once-reliable doors have quietly closed: ELISE, ELSA, EDUFI, Dora Plus, the Turing's academic breadth, and P1 internships. Fresh capacity is concentrated in new institutions (ELLIS Institute Finland, ELLIS Unit Sweden, ELAI Copenhagen, the Swiss AI Initiative) and in the UK's 2027 return to Erasmus+.

For this profile specifically, the open-source record is the asset to lead with. The groups doing the most relevant agent-security, eval and onchain-agent work all ship their research as code (AgentDojo, Inspect Evals, A1/TxRay-style tooling). A contributor who arrives with merged PRs is a cheaper, lower-risk visitor than a cold applicant. The concrete next move is three-part:

- Before Christmas 2026: open PRs and send warm-intro emails to Abdelnabi/Andriushchenko and Tramèr.
- January 2027: lock an invitation letter.
- **1 Feb 2027**: file Marietta Blau for an autumn 2027 start, keeping 1 Sep 2027 and an Erasmus+/COST combination as fallbacks.
